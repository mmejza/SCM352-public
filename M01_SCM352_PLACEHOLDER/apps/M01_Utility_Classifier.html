Key Takeaways for Gate 2

  • Performance vs. Fit: Performance attributes are directional — everyone agrees "more" is better (higher accuracy, longer range). Fit attributes are segment-specific — the preferred direction differs by customer.
  • Location vs. Timing: Both are sub-components of inconvenience (transaction costs). Location = where; timing = when. An 8AM-only service window is timing. A 3-block walk to the kiosk is location.
  • Defensible ambiguity is real: Chapter 1 acknowledges that some attributes can be legitimately classified multiple ways. On Gate 2, your justification matters as much as the label.
  • Price includes all cost components — subscription fees, per-visit costs, and hidden fees are all Price. If it costs money to obtain the service, it's price.
