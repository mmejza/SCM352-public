Someone on the supply side of every transaction you make had to decide what to stock, how many employees to schedule, where to locate a facility, and how fast to deliver — to get you what you wanted. Or to fail at it.

Operations management is the discipline that governs those decisions. Its central goal is deceptively simple: match supply with demand while making a profit.

This module introduces the foundational framework you will use throughout the course. You will learn how to analyze what customers actually want (the utility function), how firms position themselves relative to competitors (the efficient frontier), and why even well-intentioned operations so often fall short (the three system inhibitors).
