What This Course Is About

Every time you order food, rent a car, visit a doctor, or buy clothes online, you are on the demand side of an operations system. Someone on the supply side had to make dozens of decisions — what to stock, how many employees to schedule, where to locate a facility, how fast to deliver — to get you what you wanted, or to fail at it. Operations management is the discipline that governs those decisions. Its central goal is deceptively simple: match supply with demand while making a profit.

The course builds in three stages. The first five modules establish the analytical core: how to measure a process with three numbers, how to find the step that limits total output, how to improve that step efficiently, and how to handle the complexity of real operations that serve more than one type of customer. Modules 6 through 10 move from individual process analysis to operational management at scale — starting with lean thinking and the Toyota Production System, adding statistical quality control, then building an inventory management foundation from basic concepts through steady-demand EOQ models. The final three modules shift focus to planning and execution: how to build a demand forecast, how to schedule resources when multiple jobs compete for the same capacity, and how to manage the critical path of a complex one-time project.

Each module follows the same structure: read the chapter, pass a short reading check (Gate 1) to unlock lecture content and practice apps, then complete Gate 2 and the Case Analysis before the module closes.

How Canvas Is Organized
1

Always unlocked

Each module opens with the Module Overview page and the Chapter Reading link. These are available as soon as the module opens — no prerequisite.

2

Gate 1 — reading check

Gate 1 is a 5-question MCQ quiz drawn from chapter-exclusive content (not covered in slides or narration). Score 80% or higher to unlock all remaining module content. You have 2 attempts with a 24-hour cooldown between them.

3

Unlocked after Gate 1

Passing Gate 1 unlocks the lecture video segments, slide deck, all three interactive apps, the Discussion, Gate 2 Quiz, and the Case Analysis — in that sequential order.

4

Case version assignment

Your Case Analysis version (A, B, C, or D) is determined by the last digit of your student ID. Each version uses different parameter values — the questions are the same, but your numbers and your answers are your own.
