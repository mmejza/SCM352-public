How This Schedule Works
Each module has its own 6-day open window — modules open on a rolling basis and do not all close at the same time. Within each module: Day 1 = module opens, read the chapter; Day 2 = Gate 1 due at 11:59 PM (unlocks all content); Day 6 = Gate 2 and Case Analysis close at 11:59 PM. Discussion deadlines extend to the close of the following module — check the Discussion Due column below for each module's specific date. Because modules stagger, two or three will be open at the same time during most weeks. Work ahead when you can.
