Your First Five Steps
1
Get the textbook open before anything else
This course uses Cachon & Terwiesch, Operations Management. Every Gate 1 quiz is drawn exclusively from the chapter text — slides and lecture videos are not a substitute. Have the book (print or digital) accessible before you open Canvas. If you do not yet have access, email your instructor immediately — Gate 1 cannot be completed without reading the chapter first.
Do this first
2
Open Canvas → Modules → find M01
Navigate to Modules in the left sidebar. M01 is the first module. Click M01 — Module Overview to see the full item list for this module. Read through it — you will see which items are unlocked immediately and which are locked behind Gate 1. This page is your control panel for the module.
Canvas Modules → M01
3
Read Chapter 1 before taking Gate 1
Gate 1 is chapter-exclusive. All five questions test content that appears only in the chapter — not in lecture slides or video. The questions target specific numbers, definitions, and examples that skimmable readers typically miss. Budget 60–90 minutes for a thorough first read. Reading after attempting Gate 1 is an expensive way to use your second attempt.
Read first, then quiz
4
Take Gate 1 — score 80% to unlock everything
Gate 1 is a 5-question multiple-choice quiz worth 0 points. You need to score 4 of 5 (80%) to unlock the lecture slides, video, interactive apps, discussion, Gate 2, and Case Analysis. You have two attempts; if you score below 80% on Attempt 1, a second attempt unlocks after a 24-hour waiting period. Gate 1 is due Day 2 at 11:59 PM — for M01 that is Tuesday Jun 9. Waiting until Day 2 to start reading puts your second attempt at risk.
Due Tue Jun 9 · 11:59 PM
5
Note your M01 close date and open the Course Roadmap
Everything in M01 — Gate 2, Case Analysis, and your discussion reply — closes on Friday Jun 12 at 11:59 PM. That is Day 5. Open the Course Roadmap & Key Dates page (in the Course Information module) and scan the full schedule. M02 opens Wednesday Jun 10 and M03 opens Friday Jun 12 — three modules will be open simultaneously by the end of Week 1. Set calendar reminders now.
M01 closes Fri Jun 12 · 11:59 PM
