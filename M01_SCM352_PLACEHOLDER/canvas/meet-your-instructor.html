Meet Your Instructor

SCM 352: Operations Management

Hello, I'm Dr. Michael Mejza

Welcome to SCM 352. I teach operations management because I enjoy helping students develop the analytical tools to understand how organizations actually deliver goods and services — and why that is harder than it looks from the outside.

My goal is not to have you memorize operations terminology. My goal is to help you build a working toolkit: how to measure a process, identify where it breaks down, and reason carefully about how to fix it. The frameworks in this course show up in every industry, and you will use them.

A Note About SCM 352

SCM 352 is more quantitative than most business courses. You will calculate throughput rates, inventory costs, labor utilization, and critical path lengths. The course also runs on a rolling 6-day module schedule — three or four modules may be open simultaneously during peak weeks. Students who read the chapter before quizzing, complete module activities in sequence, and open each module early consistently outperform students who treat deadlines as start dates.

What You Can Expect From Me

  • Well-organized course modules with a consistent structure across all 13 modules.
  • Clear assignment instructions, grading rubrics, and version assignments for Case Analysis.
  • Timely responses to course-related questions.
  • Fair and consistent grading based on posted requirements.
  • Respectful interaction with students.

What I Expect From You

  • Read the assigned chapter before attempting Gate 1 — the quiz tests chapter content that slides do not cover.
  • Complete module items in the order they appear in Canvas: Gate 1 first, then lecture and apps, then discussion, then Gate 2 and Case Analysis.
  • Open each module early in its 6-day window — the close date is a deadline, not a suggested start time.
  • Show all work on Case Analysis submissions — partial credit requires visible reasoning, not just a final answer.
  • Ask questions when expectations or concepts are unclear, and ask before the deadline rather than after.
  • Participate professionally in discussions and course communications.
  • Take responsibility for managing the rolling schedule — modules do not wait for each other.

Communication

Email is the preferred method of communication. To help ensure a timely response, include SCM 352 and the module number in the subject line and clearly describe your question or concern.

I normally respond to emails within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends or university holidays. During periods of unusually high activity, responses may occasionally require additional time.

Please review the syllabus, module overview page, assignment instructions, grading rubric, and the FAQ page before emailing a question that may already be answered in Canvas.

Meeting With Me

If a question cannot be resolved through email, I am happy to meet with students individually. Meetings can be held in person or through video chat and are arranged by appointment.
