What is Operations Management?
Section 1.0 — core definition and competitive rationale
Core Definition

The goal of operations management is to match supply with demand while making a profit. OM focuses on designing and controlling the activities a firm performs to produce the products or services its customers want. When done well, a firm gains a significant competitive advantage over rivals.

Supply

The products or services a business offers its customers — rental cars, clothing, medical procedures.

Demand

The set of products and services customers want — a midsize sedan at O'Hare on Tuesday, a green dress in size M in Los Angeles today.
