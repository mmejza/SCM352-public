Consumption Utility: Performance and Fit
Section 1.1 — two sub-components of consumption utility
Performance

Universal Desirability

Attributes most (if not all) consumers agree are more desirable. No disagreement about which direction is "better."

Most airline passengers prefer larger seats, faster boarding, on-time departure — regardless of personal taste.

Absolute Ranking

Consumers share the same ranking: "cleaner," "more durable," "friendlier," "more memory," "more efficient."

Fit

Individual Match

Attributes that match a specific consumer's unique preferences. Consumers have heterogeneous preferences — they do not all agree on what is best.

Some prefer original Oreos; others prefer Cool Mint or peanut butter. Neither is wrong — they simply differ.

Sources of Heterogeneity

Differences in taste, color, size, ideology, dietary needs, and values — why supermarkets carry 20 cereal varieties and streaming services offer millions of songs.
