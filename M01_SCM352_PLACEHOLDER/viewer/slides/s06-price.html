Price — Second Component of Consumer Utility
Section 1.1 — price is total cost, not just the sticker
Definition

Price is the total cost of owning a product or receiving a service. It is not just the sticker price — it includes every cost a consumer incurs to own, use, and (in some cases) dispose of the product or service. Holding everything else equal, customers prefer to pay less rather than more.

What "Price" Includes

Purchase price  ·  Shipping and delivery fees  ·  Financing costs and interest  ·  Ongoing subscription or maintenance costs  ·  Trade-in value or disposal costs

Example: Smartphone

True price = device cost + monthly data plan + trade-in value when upgrading. A cheap device on an expensive plan may cost more than a premium device on a discounted plan.
