Capabilities and Strategic Trade-offs
Section 1.2 — why firms cannot excel on all utility dimensions at once
Capabilities

What a Firm Does Well

The dimensions of the customer utility function a firm is able to satisfy. No company can excel on all dimensions simultaneously — just like an athlete cannot be elite in swimming, gymnastics, and marathon running at once.

McDonald's: fast and cheap. Subway: customization. Starbucks: premium experience.

Strategic Trade-offs

No Single Set of Inputs Excels Everywhere

Firms trade off one capability dimension for another. Improving responsiveness often requires extra resources — raising costs. Better cost efficiency often requires standardization — limiting customization.

McDonald's pre-makes burgers (lower cost, faster) but cannot customize them. Subway customizes every sandwich but takes longer.

Trade-offs Create Market Segments

Because firms cannot serve everyone equally well, different firms coexist profitably by serving different customer segments with different trade-off positions.
