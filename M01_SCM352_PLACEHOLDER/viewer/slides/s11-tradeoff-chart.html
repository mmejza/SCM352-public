Strategic Trade-off: Responsiveness and Cost
Section 1.2 — Figure 1.2 · why A and B can coexist profitably
Restaurant A

$6 price  ·  10 min

Cost per customer: $4. Slower but cheaper to operate — serves cost-sensitive diners willing to wait.

Restaurant B

$7 price  ·  5 min

Cost per customer: $5. Invests in speed — serves time-sensitive diners who pay a premium.

Key Insight

Neither dominates the other on both dimensions. Each makes a trade-off to serve a distinct customer segment.
