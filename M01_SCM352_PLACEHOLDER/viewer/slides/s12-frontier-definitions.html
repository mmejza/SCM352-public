Pareto-Dominated and Efficient Frontier
Section 1.2 — three definitions students must be able to apply

Firms cannot all be equally competitive. Some are better than others in ways that leave no rational reason for customers to choose the weaker firm.

Pareto-Dominated

A firm is Pareto-dominated when at least one competitor is simultaneously better on every dimension of the customer utility function. The dominated firm provides no dimension where it has an advantage — no customer rationally prefers it.

Efficient Frontier

The set of firms in an industry that are not Pareto-dominated. Frontier firms each have at least one dimension where they outperform every other firm — giving them a customer segment who genuinely prefers them.

Critical Test

For firm X: does any other firm beat X on all dimensions simultaneously? If yes → X is Pareto-dominated. If no → X is on the efficient frontier, even if X is worst on some dimensions.
