Restaurant C: Pareto-Dominated
Section 1.2 — Figure 1.3 · dominated firms have no rational customer base
Restaurant C (Dominated)

$8 price  ·  15 min

Cost $6 — most expensive AND slowest. Both A and B are simultaneously cheaper and faster.

Why C is Dominated

A's cost ($4) < C's cost ($6) AND A's wait (10 min) < C's wait (15 min). No rational customer prefers C over A.

Inefficiency Defined

C's gap from frontier is not a trade-off — it is pure operational inefficiency caused by waste, variability, and inflexibility.
