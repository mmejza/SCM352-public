Efficient Frontier: Two Examples
Section 1.2 — Figure 1.4 · trade-off frontier vs. single-firm dominance
Left: Trade-off Frontier

A and B each excel on one dimension. C is beaten by both on every dimension — dominated. A and B coexist serving different segments.

Right: Frontier Shift

Firm E outperforms A, B, C, D on all dimensions simultaneously. E is the sole frontier firm — this is frontier-shifting innovation.

The Test

Is there any firm better on ALL dimensions? Yes → dominated. No → on the frontier, even if worst on one dimension.
