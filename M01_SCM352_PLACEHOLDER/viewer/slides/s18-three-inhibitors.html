Three System Inhibitors
Section 1.3 — Table 1.1 · what keeps firms off the efficient frontier
Waste

Consumption of inputs and resources that do not add value to the customer.

Variability

Predictable or unpredictable changes in demand or supply over time.

Inflexibility

Inability to adjust quickly or cheaply in response to variability.

Inhibitor Fast-food restaurant Rental cars Fashion retailer Emergency room
Waste Leftover food prepared but not sold Cars sitting unsold in the parking lot Items that stay in the store all season unsold Time spent on patients who could have been seen in primary care
Variability Swings in customer demand throughout the day Bad weather delaying return of rental vehicles Consumer demand driven by unpredictable fashion trends Sudden surge in patient volume during flu season
Inflexibility Rigid staffing levels that can't scale with demand Inability to move vehicles across rental center locations Long lead times to replenish fast-selling items from overseas Inability to admit patients due to lack of inpatient beds
