Flow Unit Criteria

Chapter 2 · Narration reference

A valid flow unit must be:
Relevant — tracks process output
Measurable — countable at every stage
Trackable — followable entry to exit
Consistent — same unit throughout

Reject if: it stays in the process (resource) or describes how the process runs (process characteristic).
