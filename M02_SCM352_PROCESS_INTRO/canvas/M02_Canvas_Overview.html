Introduction to Processes

Every time a customer waits too long, a shipment arrives late, or a clinic runs out of exam rooms — a process is underperforming. Operations managers fix that. But before you can fix anything, you have to measure it.

This module gives you the three measurements that define any process and the single formula that ties them together. These tools are deceptively simple. They apply to a hospital radiology unit, a subway escalator, a champagne factory, and the U.S. House of Representatives — with equal precision.

Important — module is gated. All lecture videos, apps, the discussion, Gate 2, and the Case are locked until you pass Gate 1 with an 80% or higher score. Gate 1 draws exclusively on Chapter 2 of the textbook — material that is not covered in the slides or narration. Gate 1 is due Day 2.
