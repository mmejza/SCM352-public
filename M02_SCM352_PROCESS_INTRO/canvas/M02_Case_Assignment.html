Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System operates a centralized prescription fulfillment center that fills prescriptions for 14 outpatient clinics across the region. Prescriptions arrive electronically from clinic pharmacists and move through a multi-step workflow: verification, compounding or dispensing, quality check, and packaging for courier delivery. The fulfillment director is evaluating whether the center can meet projected demand growth. She has asked you to analyze the current process metrics, identify a scope-related issue, and make a data-driven recommendation.
