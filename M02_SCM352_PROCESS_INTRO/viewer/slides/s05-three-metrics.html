The Three Process Metrics
Everything in Little's Law reduces to I, R, and T
I
Inventory
R
Flow Rate
T
Flow Time
MetricWhat It MeasuresUnitExample
I — Inventory Flow units in the process at any given time Count 45 patients in the ED
R — Flow Rate Flow units entering (or exiting) per unit of time Count / time 12 patients per hour
T — Flow Time Time one flow unit spends in the process Time / count 3.75 hours per patient

Know any two → Little's Law gives you the third.
