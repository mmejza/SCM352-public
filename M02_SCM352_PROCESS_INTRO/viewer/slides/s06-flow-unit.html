Flow Unit — Definition and Criteria
Four criteria determine whether a candidate flow unit is valid
Definition

The flow unit is the entity we track as it moves from entry to exit. Every process has exactly one flow unit — the one that aligns with the managerial question being asked.

Relevant

Tracking this unit tells you something meaningful about how well the process performs

Measurable

You can count it at every stage — arrival, in-process, and departure

Trackable

A single unit can be followed from entry to exit without ambiguity

Consistent

The same unit definition applies across all activities within the process
