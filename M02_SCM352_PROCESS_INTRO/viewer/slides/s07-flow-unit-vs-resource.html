Flow Unit vs. Resource
The most common misidentification — and how to avoid it

Flow Unit

  • Moves through the process
  • Counted as inventory (I)
  • Has a flow time (T)
  • Enters and exits the process

Car at a mechanic · Patient in a clinic · Order at a fulfillment center

Resource

  • Transforms the flow unit
  • Counted as capacity
  • Has a utilization rate
  • Stays in the process

Mechanic · Nurse · Fulfillment worker · Machine

Decision Rule

If the candidate unit stays in the process and enables output rather than becoming output — it is a resource, not a flow unit. Employees are never the flow unit.
