Little's Law
One formula — three useful forms — two pre-calculation checks
I = R × T

Solve for I

I = R × T

Known: flow rate + flow time

Solve for R

R = I ÷ T

Known: inventory + flow time

Solve for T

T = I ÷ R

Known: inventory + flow rate

Two Checks Before Calculating

1. Time units match? R in per-hour and T in minutes → convert before multiplying.
2. Flow units match? I in chairs and R in kg/week → convert R to chairs/week before dividing.
