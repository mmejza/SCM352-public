Problem Setup

A local donut shop sells 120 donuts during its 4-hour morning shift. Each donut spends an average of 30 minutes on the display rack before being sold.

Given

Total sold: 120 donuts in 4 hours
Average time on rack: 30 minutes

Find

Q1: What is the flow rate (donuts per hour)?
Q2: How many donuts are on the rack at any given time?

Formula

I = R × T  |  Check: do the time units match?
