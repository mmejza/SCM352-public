Donut Shop — Solution
Time unit conversion required before applying I = R × T
StepOperationResult
1. Flow Rate120 donuts ÷ 4 hoursR = 30 donuts/hr
2. Convert T30 min ÷ 60 min/hrT = 0.5 hr/donut
3. InventoryI = 30 donuts/hr × 0.5 hr/donutI = 15 donuts
Common Error

I = 30 × 30 = 900 — off by a factor of 60. This happens when T stays in minutes while R is in hours. Always convert to matching units first.

Takeaway

At any given moment during the shift, there are 15 donuts on the display rack — even though 120 donuts pass through in total.
