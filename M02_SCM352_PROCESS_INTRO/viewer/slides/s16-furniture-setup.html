Furniture Maker — Setup
Attempt before advancing — check flow units, not just time units
Problem Setup

A furniture workshop receives 600 kg of oak per week. Each dining chair requires 5 kg of oak. At any given time, 45 chairs are at various stages of production.

Given

Oak received: 600 kg/week
Oak per chair: 5 kg/chair
Inventory: 45 chairs

Find

Q1: What is the flow rate — chairs produced per week?
Q2: What is the average flow time per chair?

Warning

R is given in kg/week. I is in chairs. These are different flow units — you cannot use T = I ÷ R until you convert.
