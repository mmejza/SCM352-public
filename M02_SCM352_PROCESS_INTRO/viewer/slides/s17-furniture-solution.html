Convert flow units first — then apply T = I ÷ R
StepOperationResult
1. Convert R600 kg/week ÷ 5 kg/chairR = 120 chairs/week
2. Check unitsI in chairs · R in chairs/week✓ Units match
3. Flow TimeT = I ÷ R = 45 chairs ÷ 120 chairs/weekT = 0.375 weeks/chair
4. Convert T0.375 weeks × 7 days/week≈ 2.6 days/chair
Takeaway

When I and R use different flow units — convert R to match I before applying the formula. The conversion factor (5 kg/chair) is always buried in the problem setup.
