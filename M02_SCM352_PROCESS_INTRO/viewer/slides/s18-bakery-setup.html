Bakery — Setup
Attempt before advancing — identify the unit mismatch
Problem Setup

A commercial bakery produces 480 loaves per day. Each loaf spends 90 minutes in production from mixing to packaging. How many loaves are in production at any given time?

Given

Flow rate: 480 loaves/day
Flow time: 90 minutes/loaf

Find

Q1: How many loaves are in production at any given time?

Spot the Mismatch

R is in loaves/day. T is in minutes. These time units do not match — conversion required before applying I = R × T.
