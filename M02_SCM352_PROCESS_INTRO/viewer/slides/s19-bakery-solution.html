Bakery — Solution
Two valid conversion paths — same answer

Path A: Convert R to per-hour

R = 480 loaves/day ÷ 24 hr/day = 20 loaves/hr
T = 90 min ÷ 60 min/hr = 1.5 hr/loaf
I = 20 × 1.5 = 30 loaves

Path B: Convert T to days

T = 90 min ÷ (60 × 24) min/day = 0.0625 days/loaf
R = 480 loaves/day
I = 480 × 0.0625 = 30 loaves

Wrong Answer Diagnostic

I = 480 × 90 = 43,200 — this is what happens when you skip the conversion. The answer is off by a factor of 1,440 (the number of minutes in a day).

Takeaway

At any given moment, 30 loaves are in production — whether you convert R or T, the result is the same.
