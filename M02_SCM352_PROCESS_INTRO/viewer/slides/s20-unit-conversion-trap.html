The Unit Conversion Trap
Two types — both produce wrong answers that are off by a large factor

Trap 1: Time Unit Mismatch

R in per-hour · T in minutes
Wrong: I = R × T_min (off by 60×)
Right: convert T → hours first

Donut shop wrong answer: 900 instead of 15

Trap 2: Flow Unit Mismatch

I in output units · R in input units
Wrong: T = I ÷ R_raw (nonsense units)
Right: convert R → output units first

Furniture maker wrong answer: 45 chairs ÷ 600 kg = 0.075 (wrong units)

Correct Rule — Always Check Before Calculating

Step 1: What am I solving for — I, R, or T?
Step 2: Are time units the same across R and T?
Step 3: Are flow units the same across I and R?
If any answer is no → convert before applying the formula.
