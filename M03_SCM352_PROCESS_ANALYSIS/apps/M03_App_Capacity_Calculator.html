Key Concept

Chapter 4 reference

Capacity Formula
Capacity = m ÷ processing time

m = number of workers at the step

Common conversions:
sec → hr: × 3,600
min → hr: × 60
hr → day: × operating hours/day

Adding workers multiplies capacity — a station with 2 workers and a 6-min processing time has twice the capacity of one worker alone.
