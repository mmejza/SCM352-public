Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System operates a centralized prescription fulfillment center that fills prescriptions for 14 outpatient clinics across the region. The center uses a worker-paced, three-station sequential process. Your task is to complete a full process analysis — from capacity calculations through bottleneck identification, flow rate, utilization, and time-to-produce — and make a data-driven recommendation about where to add resources if patient volume grows.

Continuity note: This case extends the Riverside Health System scenario from Module 2. You now have additional data about how the center's work is organized into three discrete processing stations, and the tools to analyze what those stations can actually handle.
