The Capacity Formula
How many flow units a resource can process per unit of time
Capacity = m ÷ Processing Time

Capacity/min

C = m ÷ PT_min

PT in min/unit

Capacity/hr

C = (m ÷ PT_min) × 60

Convert to hr by ×60

From seconds

C = (m ÷ PT_sec) × 3600

Convert to hr by ×3600

VariableMeaningExample
mNumber of workers (or machines) at the station2 technicians at Station 2
PTProcessing time — time for one worker to complete one unit5 min/prescription
CCapacity of the resource in flow units per time period2/5 × 60 = 24 rx/hr
Critical: m multiplies capacity

Two workers at 5 min/unit → capacity = 2/5 = 0.4/min = 24/hr. NOT 1/5 = 0.2/min = 12/hr. Forgetting m is the single most common error in Gate 2 Q2.
