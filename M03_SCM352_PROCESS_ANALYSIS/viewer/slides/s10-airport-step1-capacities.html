Reading the Pattern

Longer processing time → lower capacity. Station 3 (1.5 min) is three times slower than Station 1 (0.5 min) — capacity is exactly one-third as large (40/hr vs. 120/hr). This relationship is direct: double the PT → halve the capacity.
