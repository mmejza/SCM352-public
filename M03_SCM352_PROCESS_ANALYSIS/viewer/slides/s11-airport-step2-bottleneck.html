Bottleneck = Station 3 (Baggage Scanner)

Process capacity = 40 travelers/hr — the minimum capacity across all stations. Stations 1 and 2 could process more, but the entire system is limited by Station 3's 40/hr rate.

Why the Bottleneck Controls Everything

Stations 1 and 2 can work faster than 40/hr — but they can only deliver travelers to Station 3 as fast as Station 3 can absorb them. Inventory (travelers waiting) builds up before Station 3. The bottleneck sets the pace for the whole process.
