Airport Security — Step 4: Utilization
Utilization = Flow Rate ÷ Resource Capacity — for every station
StationFlow RateCapacityUtilizationInterpretation
1 — ID Check 40/hr 120/hr 33.3% Busy 1 min out of every 3
2 — Body Scanner 40/hr 80/hr 50% Busy half the time
3 — Baggage Scanner 40/hr 40/hr 100% Bottleneck — always working
Reading Utilization as a Diagnostic

Bottleneck = 100% utilization → capacity-constrained. Low utilization at non-bottleneck stations reveals slack capacity — the resource is idle much of the time because it processes faster than the bottleneck can accept output.
