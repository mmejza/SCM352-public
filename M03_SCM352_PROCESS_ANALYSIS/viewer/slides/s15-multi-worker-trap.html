Multi-Worker Capacity: The Hidden Multiplier
The longest processing time is not always the bottleneck
Common But Wrong

"Station 2 takes 8 min/unit — that's the slowest station, so Station 2 must be the bottleneck." Wrong, if Station 2 has 3 workers.

StationPT (min/unit)mCapacity (units/hr)Bottleneck?
Station 1 3 min 1 1/3 × 60 = 20/hr No
Station 2 8 min ← longest 3 3/8 × 60 = 22.5/hr No
Station 3 4 min 1 1/4 × 60 = 15/hr ✓ Bottleneck
Correct Rule

Always calculate capacity (C = m ÷ PT × 60) before comparing stations. The bottleneck is the station with the lowest capacity, not the longest individual processing time.
