Problem Setup — Bakery (Already Running)

A bakery has three sequential stations: Mix (3 min/loaf, 1 baker), Bake (12 min/loaf, 1 oven), Package (1 min/loaf, 1 packer). The bakery is currently running — loaves are in process at all stations. Demand exceeds process capacity. Q = 8 additional loaves have just been ordered.

Given

Mix: 3 min/loaf, m=1
Bake: 12 min/loaf, m=1
Package: 1 min/loaf, m=1
System state: non-empty
Q = 8 loaves

Find

Q1: What is the cycle time?
Q2: How long to complete all 8 loaves from this point?

Formula for a non-empty system: Time = Q × Cycle time
