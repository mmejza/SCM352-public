Cycle time from the bottleneck · Time = Q × CT
StepOperationResult
1. CapacitiesMix=20/hr · Bake=5/hr · Package=60/hrBottleneck = Bake (5/hr)
2. Cycle time1/(5 loaves/hr) × 60 min/hrCT = 12 min/loaf
3. Time for QQ × CT = 8 × 12 min96 minutes
Interpretation

The bakery is already running. Every 12 minutes, one loaf completes. To complete 8 more loaves: 8 × 12 = 96 minutes. The cycle time is set by the oven — the bottleneck.

Why the Other Stations Don't Matter

Mixing (3 min) and packaging (1 min) are faster than baking (12 min). They sit idle waiting for the oven. The cycle time is always set by the bottleneck, regardless of how fast other steps are.
