Profit Formula

Chapter 4 · LO4-6

Profit = (flow rate × CM) − labor − fixed costs

Flow rate = min{demand, process capacity}

Improvement changes profit only if it increases flow rate. If the process is demand-constrained before AND after the improvement, flow rate is unchanged — revenue is unchanged — only costs increase.

Three-Question Check

Before approving any improvement

In order:
Q1. Capacity-constrained now?
   → No: STOP. Don't invest.
Q2. Still constrained after?
   → No: cap gain at demand.
Q3. CM justifies cost?
   → Δprofit = Δflow × CM − cost
