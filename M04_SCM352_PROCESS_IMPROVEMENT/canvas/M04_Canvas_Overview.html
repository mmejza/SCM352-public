Process Improvement

Modules 2 and 3 gave you the tools to measure a process — its metrics, its capacity, its bottleneck. This module asks the harder question: now that you know what's wrong, what do you do about it, and is it worth doing?

You will learn six levers that managers use to improve process efficiency, starting with a set of measures that expose exactly where labor is being wasted. You will calculate how many workers a process actually needs to serve its customers — not more, not less — using a discipline borrowed from music. And you will discover the counterintuitive insight that separates experienced operations managers from beginners: improving your bottleneck can have zero financial effect, and sometimes the right answer is to do nothing.

Important — module is gated. All lecture videos, apps, the discussion, Gate 2, and the Case are locked until you pass Gate 1 with an 80% or higher score. Gate 1 draws exclusively on Chapter 4 of the textbook. Gate 1 is due Day 2.
