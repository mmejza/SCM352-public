Background — All Versions

The Riverside Health System Prescription Fulfillment Center processes inpatient prescriptions through a three-step sequential process: Station 1 (prescription intake and verification), Station 2 (drug retrieval and preparation), and Station 3 (final check and dispensing). The center operates one shift of 10 hours per day, with one worker per station. The operations director has received wage and financial data and has asked you to evaluate the current efficiency of the process, determine optimal staffing, evaluate a proposed technology upgrade, and make a financial recommendation.

Your version's processing times, demand rate, wage, and financial parameters are shown in the version tabs in each part below. Use only your assigned version's data throughout the case.
