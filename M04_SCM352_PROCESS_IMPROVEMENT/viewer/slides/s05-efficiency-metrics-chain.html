Efficiency Metrics Chain
Seven metrics — computed in sequence. Each depends on the metric above it.
#MetricFormulaDepends On
1 Process capacity (each station) mi / PTi  → units per hour m = # workers at station i
2 Bottleneck Station with the lowest process capacity Step 1
3 Flow rate min { demand rate, process capacity } Steps 1–2; demand
4 Cycle time 1 / flow rate  → minutes per unit Step 3
5 Labor content Sum of all PTs across every station PT inputs only
6 Idle time & avg labor utilization Idlei = CT − PTi  ·  Util = LC / (CT × N) Steps 4–5
7 Cost of direct labor (CDL) ( N × wage ) / flow rate  → $ per unit Steps 1–3
Critical Sequence Rule

Flow rate drives everything downstream. If demand < process capacity, the process is demand-constrained — use demand as the flow rate, not process capacity.
