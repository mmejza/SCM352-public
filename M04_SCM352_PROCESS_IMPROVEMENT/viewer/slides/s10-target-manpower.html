Target Manpower
How many workers does demand require?
TM = Labor content ÷ Takt time

Labor content

Sum of all processing times (all stations). Represents total work per unit.

Takt time

The pace set by demand. Use either form — both give the same takt time.

Result

The minimum number of workers needed to meet demand. Almost never a whole number.

Rounding Rule

Round TM up to the next integer to ensure the process can meet demand. A fractional result means you cannot staff to exactly that level — you need the higher integer.

Comparison Check

After rounding: compare TM (rounded) to your current headcount. If TM > current workers → understaffed. If TM < current → overstaffed (demand-constrained signal).
