Takt Time: Same Answer, Two Routes
Artisan bakery · demand = 240 loaves per 8-hour shift
Route A — Available-Time Form

Available time = 8 hr × 60 min = 480 min

Required output = 240 loaves

Takt = 480 ÷ 240 = 2 min / loaf

Route B — Rate Form

Demand rate = 240 loaves ÷ 480 min = 0.5 loaves / min

Equivalently: 30 loaves / hr

Takt = 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2 min / loaf

Choosing Which Form to Use

If the problem gives total quantity + total time → use available-time form.
If the problem gives a rate (units/hr or units/day) → use rate form.
Both give the same answer — pick the one that requires fewer unit conversions.
