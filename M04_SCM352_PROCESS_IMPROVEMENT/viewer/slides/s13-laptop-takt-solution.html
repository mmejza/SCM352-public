Laptop Assembly Solution
100 laptops per 8-hr shift · labor content = 24 min
Available-Time Form

Available time = 8 × 60 = 480 min

Takt = 480 ÷ 100 = 4.8 min / laptop

Rate Form (Verification)

Rate = 100 ÷ 480 = 0.208/min = 12.5/hr

Takt = 1 ÷ 12.5 hr × 60 = 4.8 min ✓

TM = 24 ÷ 4.8 = 5.0 workers
Staffing Comparison

TM = 5 workers (rounded — already integer).

Current headcount = 6 workers.

Overstaffed by 1 worker. Process meets demand, but carries excess labor cost.

Key Takeaway

Both takt forms produced the same answer (4.8 min). A mismatch is always a unit error. TM < actual headcount = demand is the constraint, not capacity — the overstaffing signal you will see in the Case Analysis.
