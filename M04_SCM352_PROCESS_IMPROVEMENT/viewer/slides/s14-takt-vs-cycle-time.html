Takt Time vs. Cycle Time
The gap between demanded pace and actual pace defines the improvement agenda
Takt Time — External

Set by demand. The pace at which the market needs output.

Formula: Available time ÷ Required output (or 1 ÷ demand rate)

You cannot control takt time — you can only respond to it.

Cycle Time — Internal

Set by the bottleneck. The actual time between units leaving the process.

Formula: 1 ÷ flow rate (= 1 ÷ process capacity when capacity-constrained)

You can control cycle time through process improvement.

ScenarioWhat It MeansManagerial Action
CT > Takt Process is too slow — losing customers Line balancing, bottleneck improvement
CT = Takt Process exactly meets demand — perfect balance Maintain; watch for demand changes
CT < Takt Process is faster than demand needs — over-capacity Reduce headcount or pursue demand growth
The Managerial Insight

Takt time does not describe what the process does — it describes what the process must do. Line balancing closes the gap between cycle time and takt time.
