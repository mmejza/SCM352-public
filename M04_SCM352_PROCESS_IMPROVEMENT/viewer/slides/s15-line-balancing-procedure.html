Line Balancing Procedure
Eliminate the CT > Takt gap by redistributing activities across workers
Step 1 — Set the Target

Calculate takt time and target manpower. This tells you how many workers should handle the total labor content at the demanded pace. Use TM as your target worker count.

Step 2 — Identify the Bottleneck Worker

Find which worker's total processing time exceeds takt time. That worker is the constraint. Their activities must be redistributed until their total ≤ takt.

Step 3 — Reassign and Verify

Move the shortest feasible activity from the bottleneck worker to an adjacent worker (sequence order must be preserved). Verify: receiving worker's new total ≤ takt. Repeat until no worker exceeds takt.

Before You Balance — Check the Constraint

If demand < process capacity, the process is demand-constrained. No worker exceeds takt because the process is already slow enough. Balancing here is unnecessary and incorrect.
