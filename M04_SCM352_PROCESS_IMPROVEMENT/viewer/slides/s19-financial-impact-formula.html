Financial Impact Framework
Does a bottleneck improvement increase profit? Depends on the constraint.
Profit ($/hr) = Flow rate × Contribution margin − Labor cost − Fixed cost

Contribution Margin

Revenue per unit − variable cost per unit. The profit contribution of each additional unit processed.

Flow Rate (key variable)

min{ demand, process capacity }. This is the only term that changes when you improve the bottleneck.

Fixed + Labor Costs

These are unchanged by a bottleneck improvement in the short run — they are fixed per hour regardless of throughput.

The Improvement Logic

Capacity-constrained: Process capacity < demand. Improving the bottleneck → process capacity increases → flow rate increases → profit increases by (Δ flow rate × CM).

Gross Margin

Gross margin = Contribution margin ÷ Revenue per unit. A measure of profitability per unit before fixed costs. Defined in Chapter 4 — relevant to Gate 2 Q6 CDL analysis.
