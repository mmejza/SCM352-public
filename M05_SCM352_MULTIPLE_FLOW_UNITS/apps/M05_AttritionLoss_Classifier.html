Backward Chain Rules

Chapter 5 — attrition loss

Work downstream → upstream:
D(last) = 1 ÷ ylast
D(i) = D(i+1) ÷ yi

Then:
Capacity = 1 ÷ PT (units/min)
IU = D ÷ capacity · bottleneck = max IU
Process capacity = 1 ÷ max IU (good/min) × 60
Inputs needed = Q ÷ process yield (decimal!)
