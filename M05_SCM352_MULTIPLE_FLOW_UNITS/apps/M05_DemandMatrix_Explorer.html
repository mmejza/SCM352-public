Five-Step Procedure

Chapter 5 sequence

Same five steps, every time:
1. Demand matrix (zeros = routing)
2. Total demand = row sums
3. IU = total demand ÷ capacity
4. Bottleneck = highest IU
5. Max IU > 100% → flow = demand ÷ max IU
    Max IU ≤ 100% → flow = demand
