Workload Rules

Chapter 5 — minutes, not units

The sequence:
Workload(i, j) = demandj × PT(i, j)
Total workload = row sum (min/hr)
Available time = workers × 60
IU = workload ÷ available time
Max IU > 100% → flow = demand ÷ max IU

Staffing changes available time.
Demand changes workload.
Never trade places.
