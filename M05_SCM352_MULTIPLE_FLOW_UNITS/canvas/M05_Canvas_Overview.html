Process Analysis with Multiple Flow Units

Every module so far has assumed a single type of flow unit flowing through a single path. That assumption held for three-station pharmacy processes and single-product assembly lines. It breaks down the moment your operation handles more than one kind of customer, product, or case.

A loan approval office rejects half its applications before they even reach quality assurance. An urgent care clinic routes some patients to imaging, some to labs, and others to the doctor only. A prescription fulfillment center now fills both standard prescriptions and pre-packaged unit-dose medications — and those two types don't visit the same stations.

This module gives you the tools to handle these realities: a demand matrix to map who goes where, implied utilization to find the true bottleneck when flow units take different paths, process yield to account for defects and attrition, and a workload matrix to handle different processing times for different types. These tools don't replace what you learned in Modules 3 and 4 — they extend it.
