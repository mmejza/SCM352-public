Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System has recently added pre-filled unit-dose medications from specialty manufacturers to its outpatient formulary. These prescriptions — called Type 2 (Unit-Dose) — arrive from the manufacturer already measured, prepared, and sealed. They require only verification by a pharmacist and a final QC/packaging check before delivery. They skip the Drug Selection and Preparation station entirely. Standard prescriptions remain Type 1 and visit all three stations as before.

The center operates the same three-station sequential process from Modules 3 and 4, one worker per station:

  • Station 1 — Prescription Verification: pharmacist reviews completeness, drug interactions, identity. Both types visit.
  • Station 2 — Drug Selection and Preparation: technician retrieves and prepares medication. Type 2 skips this station.
  • Station 3 — QC and Packaging: final safety check and packaging for delivery. Both types visit.

Continuity note: this case extends the Riverside scenario from Modules 2–4. You do not need your prior module results — all relevant data is below.
