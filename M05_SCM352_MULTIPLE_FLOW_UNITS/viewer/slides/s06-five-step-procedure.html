The Five-Step Procedure
Same five steps, every product-mix process — memorize the order
StepActionOutput
1Construct the demand matrixWho goes where — zeros where a type skips a resource
2Total demand per resourceRow sums — all types arriving at each resource
3Implied utilization per resourceIU = total demand ÷ capacity
4Check the constraintMax IU > 100% → capacity-constrained; otherwise demand-constrained
5Determine flow ratesPer type — rule depends on Step 4 (next slides)
Decision Rule

The bottleneck is the resource with the highest implied utilization — found in Step 3, never by comparing processing times.
