Utilization vs. Implied Utilization
One looks backward at what happened — the other looks at what demand requires
Utilization (M03–M04)
Utilization = flow rate ÷ capacity
  • • Never exceeds 100%
  • • Measures how busy a resource is
  • • Can look comfortable while demand goes unserved
Implied Utilization (M05)
IU = total demand ÷ capacity
  • • Can exceed 100%
  • • Measures how busy a resource would need to be to serve all demand
  • • IU = 150% → resource would need 1.5× its capacity
Bottom Takeaway

In a product mix, only implied utilization finds the bottleneck. Utilization cannot — it is capped at 100% precisely when the constraint binds.
