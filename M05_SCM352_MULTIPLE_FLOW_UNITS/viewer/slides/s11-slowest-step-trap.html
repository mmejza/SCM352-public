Trap — "The Slowest Resource Is the Bottleneck"
True in Module 03. False the moment flow units take different paths.
Why It Seems Correct

In a single-flow process, every unit visits every resource — so the lowest-capacity resource is always the most loaded. M03 trained you to look for it.

Why It Is Wrong Here

With a product mix, resources face different demand. In the print shop: R2 has the lowest capacity (8/hr) but only 6 jobs/hr arrive (IU 0.75). R3 has 50% more capacity — and is overwhelmed (IU 1.167), because both types route through it.

Correct Rule

Bottleneck = the resource with the highest implied utilization. Capacity alone tells you nothing until you know who routes through the resource.
