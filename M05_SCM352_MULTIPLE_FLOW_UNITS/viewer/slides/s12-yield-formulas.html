Yield & Process Yield
When units drop out, count what survives — then work backward
Yield (resource i)  =  good output at i ÷ input at i
Process yield  =  y1 × y2 × … × yn
Demand per good unit (backward):   D(last) = 1 ÷ ylast ,   D(i) = D(i+1) ÷ yi
Two Things to Notice

1  A phase with 100% yield still appears in the backward chain — dividing by 1.0 changes nothing, but skipping the phase scrambles the order.   2  Yields multiply as decimals: 95% × 85% is 0.95 × 0.85, never 95 × 85.

Why Work Backward

The backward chain answers: "to deliver 1 good unit at the end, how many must each phase start?" Those D values are the demand matrix of an attrition process — and they find its bottleneck.
