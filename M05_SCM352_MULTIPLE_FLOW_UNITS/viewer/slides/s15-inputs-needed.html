Inputs Needed for a Target Output
One formula — and one error that destroys it
Inputs needed  =  Q  ÷  process yield (decimal form)
Bakery Example

Target Q = 100 good cakes. Process yield = 0.646.   Inputs needed = 100 ÷ 0.646 = 154.8 cakes started. Nearly 55 extra starts to cover attrition.

The Decimal Trap

Dividing by the percentage — 100 ÷ 64.6 = 1.55 — produces an answer smaller than the target. You cannot start 1.55 cakes and finish 100.

Sanity Check — Every Time

Inputs needed must always be greater than Q. If your answer is smaller than the target, you divided by the percentage instead of the decimal. Convert first: 64.6% → 0.646.
