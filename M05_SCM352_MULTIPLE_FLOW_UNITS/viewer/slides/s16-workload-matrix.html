The Workload Matrix
When types take different amounts of time, count minutes — not units
When to Switch

Ten fast units and ten slow units are not the same burden. The moment processing times depend on the flow unit type, the demand matrix in units stops measuring load — the workload matrix in minutes takes over.

Workload (i, j)  =  demandj × processing time (i, j)   min/hr
Available time (i)  =  workers at i × 60   min/hr
IU (i)  =  total workload at i ÷ available time at i
Two Things to Notice

1  Zeros still mark routing — a type that skips a resource contributes 0 minutes there.   2  Staffing enters only through available time. After the IU row, Steps 4–5 are identical to the five-step procedure.
