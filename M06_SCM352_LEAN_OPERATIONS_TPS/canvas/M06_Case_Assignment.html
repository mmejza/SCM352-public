Background — All Versions

This case extends the Riverside Health System scenario from Modules 2–5. The Riverside prescription fulfillment center is now audited for lean performance across five dimensions. You will apply the full lean framework from Module 6 to a healthcare operations context.

The Riverside Prescription Center operates as an outpatient pharmacy serving patients of the Riverside Health System network. Prescriptions arrive throughout the day, are processed by a team of pharmacy technicians under pharmacist supervision, and are either dispensed in-center or prepared for mail-out delivery.

The center manager has engaged the operations team to conduct a lean audit — identifying where value is and is not being created, calculating key lean metrics, and proposing targeted improvements. As the analyst, you will complete that audit using the framework and formulas from Module 6.
