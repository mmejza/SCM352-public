Instructions

Before You Begin

  • Have your Chapter 8 notes, slides, and lecture notes ready before you click Begin — this quiz is open-book and open-notes.
  • Have scratch paper ready. Questions 1–5 and 7 require written calculations that you will transcribe into Canvas.
  • The timer starts immediately when you click Begin and does not pause for any reason.
  • Do not refresh the page or navigate away during the quiz — Canvas will not save an in-progress answer if you leave a question field mid-entry.
  • Questions 2 and 5 will each display a data table with your version's scenario. Both questions use the same scenario — treat them as one analysis split across two questions.
  • Question 7 also uses the same scenario as Q2 and Q5. Reference those data tables when answering Q7.

Answering Each Question Type

  • Calculation questions (Q1–Q5, Q7): Show the formula, substitute all values, and state the final result. A numerically correct answer without work shown earns a maximum of 50% of the question's points.
  • Essay questions (Q6, Q8): All sub-parts must be answered. Vague or generic answers receive partial or no credit. Use Chapter 8 vocabulary precisely — "waiting waste," "mura," "heijunka," "kanban signal" are terms with specific meanings; don't substitute informal paraphrases.
  • Sub-part structure: Most questions have labeled sub-parts (a), (b), (c). Answer each sub-part explicitly. Do not combine answers into a single paragraph — the grader needs to award points per sub-part.
  • Units matter: Include units in every calculation answer (minutes, units/day, workers, etc.). Answers without units on calculation questions are treated as incomplete.
Grading and Timing

Grading turnaround: Calculation and essay questions are manually graded. Grades are returned within 5 business days of the quiz close date.

Technical issues: If Canvas freezes, closes, or you experience a technical failure during the quiz, contact your instructor immediately with a screenshot or description of the error. Do not attempt to re-enter the quiz — doing so will consume your one attempt. Documented technical issues may be eligible for an instructor-authorized reset.

No grade disputes on calculation errors: If you show correct work but make an arithmetic error that propagates forward, penalties are applied once at the origin. If you do not show work and get the wrong answer, no partial credit is available — this policy is fixed.
