Two Lenses, Two Questions
OEE and value-added % measure different things — neither replaces the other
OEE Lens — The Resource

"How is the worker's / machine's time being used?"

Unit of analysis: the worker, machine, or station

OEE = VA time ⁄ Total available shift time

Denominator: shift time — total scheduled time the resource is available

"What fraction of this person's shift created value?"

VA% Lens — The Flow Unit

"How is the order's / patient's / claim's time being used?"

Unit of analysis: the order, patient, claim, or product

VA% = VA time ⁄ Total flow time

Denominator: flow time — total time the unit spends in the system

"What fraction of the unit's journey created value?"

The Critical Distinction

High OEE + low VA%: workers are busy, but the product waits between them. Low OEE + moderate VA%: workers have idle time, but the product moves efficiently when work is done. Lean addresses both — with different tools.
