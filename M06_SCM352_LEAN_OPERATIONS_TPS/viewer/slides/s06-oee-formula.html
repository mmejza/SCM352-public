OEE — Overall Equipment Effectiveness
Measuring the fraction of the resource's shift that creates value
OEE = Value-Added Time ⁄ Total Available Shift Time
Numerator — VA Time

Time the resource is performing work that directly creates value for the customer — transforming the product, treating the patient, processing the claim.

Denominator — Shift Time

Total scheduled time the resource is available — includes VA time, all NVA waste, and NVA-necessary time. This is not flow time.

Two Things to Notice

1. NVA-necessary time is included in the denominator (it uses the resource's time) but not the numerator (it doesn't create customer value). This is a frequent error.

2. OEE below 60% means more than 40% of the resource's time is absorbed by waste. World-class manufacturing targets 85%+. Most services operate below 65%.

Goal

Not 100% OEE — some NVA-necessary time is unavoidable. The goal is to eliminate the NVA waste portion while preserving NVA-necessary steps.
