NVA-Necessary Trap

Regulatory documentation (60 min) goes in the denominator — it uses shift time.

It does not go in the numerator — it creates no customer value.

Adding it to the numerator → OEE = 260/480 = 54.2% → wrong.

Key Takeaway

Largest waste: Waiting (90 min, 18.75% of shift). Targeted intervention: physician order coordination or a proactive alert system to reduce nurse idle time between orders.

OEE = 41.7% means 58.3% of the nurse's shift is non-value-adding.
