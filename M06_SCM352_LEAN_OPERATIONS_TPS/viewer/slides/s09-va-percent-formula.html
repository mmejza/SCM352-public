Value-Added % — The Flow Unit's Perspective
Measuring the fraction of the unit's journey through the process that creates value
VA% = Value-Added Time ⁄ Total Flow Time
Numerator — VA Time

Time the flow unit is being actively transformed — work is being done to it. The unit is moving through the process.

Denominator — Flow Time

Total elapsed time from the unit's entry to its exit from the process. Includes all wait times, queues, and delays.

Not the shift time. Not the worker's available time.

The Denominator Trap

Using shift time as the denominator gives you OEE — not VA%.

Using flow time gives you VA%.

This is the most common error when students apply VA% for the first time.

Why It Matters

A process with VA% = 10% means the unit spends 90% of its time waiting — even if the workers are fully occupied. VA% is the customer's experience of the process, not the worker's.
