Same Process — Two Different Numbers
Adding the worker's perspective changes everything
The Same DMV — Now With Clerk Data

The DMV clerk works an 8-hour (480-min) shift. Of that time, she spends 300 minutes actively processing licenses. The remaining 180 minutes is required training, breaks, and administrative tasks.

Clerk's OEE
OEE = 300 ⁄ 480 = 62.5%

"What fraction of the clerk's shift was value-creating?"

62.5% — the clerk is relatively productive for a service worker.

Customer's VA%
VA% = 15 ⁄ 80 = 18.75%

"What fraction of the customer's time was value-creating?"

18.75% — the customer wastes 81% of their visit waiting.

The Insight

These are not contradictory — they measure different things. The clerk's idle time and the customer's wait time are not the same thing. Lean must address both: reduce the clerk's NVA waste and reduce the customer's flow-time waste — with different interventions.
