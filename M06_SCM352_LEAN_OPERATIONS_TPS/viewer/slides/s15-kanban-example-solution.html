Auto Parts Kanban — Solution
D=200 · LT=3 days · SS=100 · C=50 units/container
With Safety Stock

D × LT = 200 × 3 = 600 units (demand during LT window)

+ SS = 100 units → total = 700 units

K = 700 ⁄ 50 = 14 containers

Total authorized inventory: 14 × 50 = 700 units

Without Safety Stock (SS = 0)

D × LT = 600 units → ÷ 50 = 12 containers

SS adds exactly 2 containers — the buffer against LT uncertainty.

Lead Time Reduction Insight

If LT drops from 3 days to 1 day (same demand, same SS):

K = (200 × 1 + 100) / 50 = 300 / 50 = 6 containers

From 14 → 6 containers: 57% reduction in authorized inventory from a single lead time improvement.

Key Takeaway

Lead time reduction is an inventory reduction strategy. Shorter supplier LT → fewer containers needed → less capital tied up in WIP. Lean targets LT, not just output.
