Takt Time & Target Manpower
Matching production pace and staffing to customer demand
Formula 1 — Takt Time
Takt = Available production time ⁄ Demand rate

Unit: time per unit (min/unit, hr/unit)

Interpretation: "How often must we finish one unit to keep pace with customers?"

Key: Takt is set by demand — you cannot control it, only respond to it.

Formula 2 — Target Manpower
TM = Total labor content ⁄ Takt time

Unit: workers

Interpretation: "How many workers are needed to hit takt time exactly?"

Round UP to the nearest whole number — you cannot staff 0.7 of a worker.

Demand Increases → Takt Falls → TM Rises

More customers demand faster output (shorter takt). Shorter takt with the same labor content requires more workers. TM rises in direct proportion to the demand increase. This is a lean staffing decision — add exactly the number of workers required, not more.
