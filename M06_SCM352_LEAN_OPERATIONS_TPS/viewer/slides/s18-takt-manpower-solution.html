Laptop Manufacturing — Solution
420 min/shift · 70 laptops/day → 84 laptops/day · LC = 24 min
Baseline: 70 laptops/day

Takt = 420 ⁄ 70 = 6.0 min/laptop

TM = 24 ⁄ 6.0 = 4.0 workers (exact — no rounding)

4 workers × 6 min each = 24 min labor content per unit ✓

Demand Increases to 84 laptops/day

New takt = 420 ⁄ 84 = 5.0 min/laptop

New TM = 24 ⁄ 5.0 = 4.8 → round UP to 5 workers

Management must add 1 worker.

Why Round Up?

TM = 4.8 means 4 workers cannot meet takt. Each of the 4 workers processes at 24/4 = 6 min — but takt is now 5 min. The line falls behind demand.

5 workers process at 24/5 = 4.8 min/unit < 5.0 takt. Line meets demand. One worker has a small amount of slack — that is acceptable.

Key Takeaway

This is lean staffing, not intuitive staffing. Don't add people until you're "fast enough" — add exactly the number required to match takt time. That precision is the discipline.
