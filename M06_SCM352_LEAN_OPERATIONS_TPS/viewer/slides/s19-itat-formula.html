ITAT — Information Turnaround Time
How long before a defect is detected and the operator gets feedback?
ITAT = Cycle time of detection step × Buffer (units between defect and detection steps)
Three Terms

Defect-generation step: the process step where errors are introduced — where the root cause originates

Detection step: the step where errors are first identified and caught

Buffer: the number of units queued between the defect step and the detection step

Unit Check

CT × buffer = (min/unit) × (units) = minutes

ITAT is a time — how many minutes (or hours, days) elapse between when a defect is made and when it's caught.

What ITAT Reveals

A high ITAT means the operator won't know about an error for a long time. During that window, every unit processed at the defect step may carry the same error.

The longer ITAT is, the more defective units are produced before detection.

To Reduce ITAT

Reduce the buffer between defect-generation and detection. Lean's push toward single-unit flow is, at its core, a quality strategy — not just an inventory strategy.
