Engine Assembly — ITAT Solution
Step 3 defect (bolt torque) · Step 9 detection · CT=5 min · Buffer=8 units
Baseline ITAT
ITAT = 5 × 8 = 40 minutes

When a torque error is made at Step 3, it will not be detected until 40 minutes later — after 8 units have passed through Steps 4–8.

If the wrong torque setting has been used for 1 hour → up to 12 units may carry the defect before detection.

Buffer Reduced to 2 Units
ITAT = 5 × 2 = 10 minutes

75% reduction in detection time from a single buffer reduction.

The Quality Learning Mechanism

ITAT is not just damage control. With ITAT = 10 min, the operator gets feedback while the error is fresh — they can trace the root cause before 8 more units are built wrong.

With ITAT = 40 min, the operator may have forgotten the specific actions at Step 3 — root cause tracing is harder, and the fix is slower.

Key Takeaway

Large buffers are invisible defect amplifiers. They don't cause errors — but they multiply the damage from every error that occurs. Single-unit flow minimizes ITAT and maximizes the quality feedback loop.
