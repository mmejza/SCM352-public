The 3Ms — Reading Waste at the System Level
Mura causes Muri causes Muda — targeting Muda alone does not fix the system
MURA — Unevenness

Irregular, inconsistent demand or production pace

Example: 300 units Monday, 50 units Thursday, 180 units Friday

Root cause: batching decisions, end-of-period pushes, lumpy customer orders

MURI — Overburden

Resources pushed beyond their capacity or capability

Example: overtime Monday, idle workers Thursday, rushed quality Friday

Caused by Mura — unevenness forces peaks of overburden

MUDA — Waste

The seven sources of non-value-adding activity

Example: defects from rushed Monday production; idle time Thursday; motion waste from disorganized Friday surge

Generated by Muri — overburden produces waste

The Causal Direction

Lean interventions targeting muda alone are symptomatic — the waste recurs because its source (muri) remains. Sustainable improvement traces muda back through muri to its mura source, then eliminates the unevenness. Heijunka (demand leveling) attacks mura directly.
