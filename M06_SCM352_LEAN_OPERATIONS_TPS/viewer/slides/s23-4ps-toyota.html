The 4Ps of Toyota
Liker's Toyota Way — four interdependent layers of the lean enterprise
4
PROBLEM SOLVING — Top Layer

Genchi genbutsu (go and see); root cause analysis (5 Whys); kaizen (continuous improvement); organizational learning through structured experimentation

3
PEOPLE AND PARTNERS

Developing employees as problem-solvers, not just producers; long-term supplier relationships; growing leaders from within; cross-training for flexibility

2
PROCESSES — Where Heijunka Lives

Continuous flow; pull systems (kanban); level production (heijunka); stop to fix problems (jidoka); standardized work as the baseline for kaizen

1
PHILOSOPHY — Foundation

Long-term thinking as the foundation; patient-capital approach to improvement investments; "we will build a learning organization that continuously improves"

Gate 2 Q8(d)

Which P does a heijunka redesign reflect? Answer: Processes — heijunka is a production system design decision, not a philosophy, people practice, or problem-solving technique.
