Quality and Statistical Process Control

In September 1994, the MS Estonia — a modern cruise ferry with experienced crew — sank in the Baltic Sea, killing more than 800 people. None of the individual problems that day was unusual. Together, they were fatal. This module gives you the statistical framework for understanding how variation combines into failure — and what operations can do to prevent it.

Quality management is the discipline of understanding why variation — small, ordinary, individual variation — sometimes combines into catastrophic failure and what operations can do to prevent it. This module gives you a rigorous statistical framework for answering that question.

You will learn to measure whether a process is even capable of meeting its specifications before you monitor it, how to build control charts that detect when something genuinely wrong has entered the process versus when randomness is just doing what randomness does, how to trace the root causes of defects using fishbone diagrams and Pareto analysis, and how to model the probability that a defect escapes a multi-layered quality system.
