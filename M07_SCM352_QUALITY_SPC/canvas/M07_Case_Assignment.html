Background — All Versions

This case extends the Riverside Health System scenario from Modules 2–5. The fulfillment center now implements a formal quality monitoring program. You will apply the SPC framework to two quality concerns: prescription fill accuracy (a continuous outcome) and prescription dispensing errors (a binary outcome).

The Riverside center fills prescriptions for a critical medication. Each prescription must contain exactly the target amount of active ingredient (see your version parameters). The engineering specification allows a tolerance band. The center also tracks whether prescriptions are dispensed with errors — wrong patient, wrong drug, wrong dose — a binary outcome. A three-stage quality safeguard system catches errors before they reach patients.
