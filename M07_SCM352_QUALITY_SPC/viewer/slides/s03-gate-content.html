1 · Section 9.6 — Event Trees (pp. 278–279)

AND-logic: any failure = defect. P(Good) = P(Step 1 correct) × P(Step 2 correct) × ...

OR/robust-logic: all safeguards must fail. P(Defect) = P(check 1 misses) × P(check 2 misses) × ...
