Process Capability Index — Cp
Measures how well the process variation fits within the engineering specification
Cp = (USL − LSL) / (6σ̂)
Tolerance width divided by six standard deviations of the process
Components

USL = Upper specification limit (engineering/customer requirement)

LSL = Lower specification limit

σ̂ = Estimated process standard deviation (from production data)

6σ̂ = Width capturing ±3σ of process output

Two Things to Notice

Numerator (USL−LSL): Fixed by the specification. Management cannot widen the tolerance without relaxing quality standards.

Denominator (6σ̂): Controlled by process improvement. Reducing σ̂ is the only lever to increase Cp while holding the specification constant.

The Algebra

To double Cp: double (USL−LSL)/6Cp_target = σ̂_required. Since the numerator is fixed, doubling Cp requires halving σ̂. Gate 2 Q1(c) tests this directly.
