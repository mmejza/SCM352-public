Interpreting Cp — Three-Threshold Rule
Three zones: each requires a different operational response
Cp ≥ 1.33
"Surely meets the design specification"

Process variation fits comfortably within the tolerance interval. Traditional quality standard. Deploy control chart to monitor consistency.

1.00 ≤ Cp < 1.33
"Uncertain capability"

Process may or may not consistently meet specs. Not clearly defective, not robustly capable. Reduce σ̂ further before deploying control chart.

Cp < 1.00
"Surely does NOT meet the design specification"

Process variation exceeds the tolerance interval. Producing defects from common cause variation. Process redesign required before any control chart.

Gate 2 Q1 Language Requirement

State the exact threshold that applies, not a paraphrase. "Uncertain capability" is the required term — not "borderline" or "close to meeting specs." The threshold range matters: identify whether Cp falls into ≥1.33, [1.00, 1.33), or <1.00.
