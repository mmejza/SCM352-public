Pharmaceutical Fill — Cp Calculation

Problem Setup

A pharmaceutical company fills capsules that must contain exactly 500 mg of active ingredient.

Specification: Tolerance of ±30 mg

LSL = 470 mg  ·  USL = 530 mg

Process: Estimated standard deviation σ̂ = 8 mg

Cp = (USL − LSL) / (6σ̂)
Q1 — Calculate Cp

Show the formula, plug-in values, and result.

Q2 — Classify the Process

Using the three-threshold rule, classify this process. State the threshold and what it means for the pharmaceutical company.

Q3 — Target Standard Deviation

Management wants to reach Cp = 1.33 without changing the specification. What maximum σ̂ is allowable? Show the algebra.

Common setup error: computing Cp = 60/8 instead of 60/48. The denominator is 6σ̂, not σ̂ alone. Write out 6 × 8 = 48 before dividing.
