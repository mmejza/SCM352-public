Pharmaceutical Fill — Cp Solution
LSL = 470 mg · USL = 530 mg · σ̂ = 8 mg
Q1 — Cp Calculation
Cp = (530 − 470) / (6 × 8) = 60 / 48 = 1.250
Q2 — Classification

1.00 < Cp = 1.250 < 1.33 → Uncertain capability

The fill process is tighter than the absolute minimum (Cp > 1.00) but does not meet the traditional standard (Cp < 1.33). The company cannot be confident the process will consistently meet the ±30 mg specification.

Q3 — Target σ̂

Target: Cp = 1.33

1.33 = 60 / (6σ̂)
6σ̂ = 60 / 1.33 = 45.1
σ̂max = 7.52 mg

Reduce σ̂ from 8 mg to below 7.52 mg to reach the traditional standard.

Key Takeaway

Uncertain capability = the process is borderline. Not clearly producing defects, but not robustly capable. The correct response is process improvement — not deploying a control chart on a marginally capable process.
