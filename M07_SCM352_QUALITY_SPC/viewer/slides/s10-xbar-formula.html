X-bar Control Chart — Construction Formulas
Three-step build: sample means → grand mean → ESD → control limits
X̄ = (x₁ + x₂ + … + xₙ) / n
Sample mean for one period · n = number of observations per period
X̄̄ = (X̄₁ + X̄₂ + … + X̄ₘ) / m
Grand mean (center line) across all m sample periods
ESD(X̄) = σ(all parts) / √n
σ(all parts) = std dev of ALL individual measurements from ALL samples combined · n = sample size per period
UCL = X̄̄ + 3 × ESD(X̄)     LCL = X̄̄ − 3 × ESD(X̄)
Control limits define the expected range of sample means under normal process variation
Critical — Most Common Gate 2 Error

σ(all parts) ≠ std dev of X̄ values. σ(all parts) is the standard deviation computed across every individual measurement from every period combined. The n in the formula is the sample size per period — not the number of periods. Gate 2 Q2 requires showing the formula explicitly before substituting values.
