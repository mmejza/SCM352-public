Bank Deposit Slips — p-Chart Solution
n = 200 · 8 days · Total defective = 54
Step 1 — Centerline
p̄ = 54 / (8 × 200) = 54 / 1,600 = 0.03375

Total defects / total items — not average of daily p values

Step 2 — ESD(p)
ESD = √(0.03375 × 0.96625 / 200)
= √(0.0001630)
= 0.01277
Step 3 — Control Limits
UCL = 0.03375 + 3(0.01277) = 0.07206
LCL = 0.03375 − 3(0.01277) = −0.00456 → 0

LCL is negative — set to 0 (proportion cannot be negative)

Day 9 — Out of Control Signal
p₉ = 18 / 200 = 0.090

0.090 > UCL (0.07206) → Out of Control

Day 9 proportion (9.0%) significantly exceeds the control limit (7.2%). This signals an assignable cause — investigate before Day 10 processing.

The Decision is Mechanical

Do not eyeball the chart. Compute UCL, compute the new p. If new p exceeds UCL, investigate. 0.090 > 0.07206 → investigation required regardless of subjective impression.
