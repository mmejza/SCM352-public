Control Limits ≠ Specification Limits
Two independent standards · one statistical · one managerial
Control Limits

What they are: Computed from actual process data

What they mean: Define the expected range of natural variation when the process is stable

Who sets them: Statistical calculation (X̄̄ ± 3 × ESD)

Question they answer: "Is the process behaving consistently?"

In Control

All sample means fall between UCL and LCL. No assignable cause detected. Does not mean the process meets customer requirements.

Specification Limits

What they are: Set by engineering design or customer requirements

What they mean: Define the acceptable range for the product or service

Who sets them: Engineers, customers, or regulations (LSL / USL)

Question they answer: "Does the output meet requirements?"

The Dangerous Case — Gate 2 Q4

In control but incapable (Cp = 0.83): The process is stable and predictable — but it is predictably producing some output outside specification. Control chart says "no assignable cause." Cp says "the process cannot reliably meet the spec."

Response: process improvement, not just monitoring.
