Background — All Versions

This case extends the Riverside Health System scenario used in Modules 2 through 7. The focus now shifts to the Central Supply department, which manages the inventory of medical and surgical supplies distributed to nursing units throughout the hospital system. You will apply Chapter 10 concepts to measure Riverside's inventory performance, evaluate holding costs, and analyze the consequences of its inventory decisions.

Riverside Central Supply manages inventory of medical and surgical supplies across its regional hospital network. The department purchases supplies from distributors at cost and tracks inventory in cost dollars. Your version includes specific financial and operational data for Riverside's supply categories. You will analyze the Central Supply department as a process — supplies flow in from distributors, are held in inventory, and flow out to nursing units and surgical suites.
