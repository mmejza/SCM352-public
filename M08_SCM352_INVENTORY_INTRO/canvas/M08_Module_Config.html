Step 4 — During-Module Monitoring

Weekly monitoring actions — 30–45 min total

Day 3
Pull Gate 1 completion report. Students with 0 attempts by Day 3 should receive a targeted nudge. M08-specific: Gate 1 questions draw exclusively on Tables 10.1, 10.2, 10.3, the Monitor Sugar/production-smoothing content, and Chapter 10 numerical benchmarks — none of which appear in slides or narration. Students who only watched lectures will fail Gate 1. Do not message the whole class; nudge individual students with 0 attempts.
Day 5
Scan discussion initial posts for naming violations. M08-specific watch items: (1) Posts that name a textbook example by name — Kroger, Macy's, Monitor Sugar are the most common; (2) Posts describing only a consumer experience without applying the Ch.10 classification framework (inventory type, reason from the six) — likely Minimal on Criterion 1; (3) Posts that estimate turns/DoS without citing Table 10.1 or 10.2 — partial credit on Criterion 2. Apply 10-pt naming penalty to the post score before criterion-level grading.
Day 6
After Gate 2 closes at 11:59 PM: Export quiz statistics. M08-specific error patterns to watch: (a) Q4 — answers off by a factor of 7 (student inverted weekly turns and reported the result as "days" rather than "weeks"); (b) Q5 — turns 1.5–2× the expected value (used revenue instead of COGS); (c) Q7 — per-unit holding cost equals the annual HCP × unit cost without dividing by turns (applied annual HCP directly). These indicate formula confusion, not arithmetic errors — address in Module Wrap-Up announcement.
Post-module
Grade Discussion initial posts after following-module close. No peer reply required — grade initial posts only. Use M08_Discussion_Rubric.html. Recommend grading in criterion order (Criterion 4 first — most diagnostic) rather than post-by-post. Grade all Criterion 4 responses across all students before moving to Criterion 3. Estimated time: 2–4 min per student.
