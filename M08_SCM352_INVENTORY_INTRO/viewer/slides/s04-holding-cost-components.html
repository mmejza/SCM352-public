The Four Holding Cost Components
What you pay every day inventory sits on a shelf
Component What it represents Visible in budget? When it appears
Opportunity cost of capital The income the firm could have earned by investing the capital tied up in inventory elsewhere No Income never earned — invisible until compared
Warehouse / storage cost Rent, utilities, and labor to store and handle inventory in a warehouse or storage facility Yes Shows as a line item in operating budget
Obsolescence / markdown risk The probability that the item loses value due to technological change, fashion shift, or expiration before it sells No Appears only at the markdown or write-off event
Spoilage / shrinkage Physical loss from perishability, theft, or damage while the item is in storage or handling No Discovered at inventory count — loss already occurred
Annual Holding Cost Percentage Formula
Annual HCP% = (Sum of all four component costs per year) ÷ Unit cost × 100

Actual holding cost per unit = Unit cost × (Annual HCP% ÷ Annual turns)
