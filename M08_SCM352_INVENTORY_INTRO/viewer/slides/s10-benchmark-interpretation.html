Benchmark Interpretation: Two Approaches
Descriptive vs. analytical — which one gets assessment credit
Wrong Use — Descriptive Only
  1. Look up the segment average in Table 10.1 or 10.2
  2. Compare your company's turns to the average
  3. Declare the company "good" or "bad"

This gets the number right but misses the analysis point — no credit for the evaluation component.

Right Use — Analytical
  1. Look up the segment average
  2. Compare your company's turns to the average
  3. Identify the structural factors that explain the benchmark level
  4. Ask whether those factors apply to this company's specific situation

This earns the analysis credit.

The Key Distinction

Benchmarks tell you WHERE a company sits relative to peers. They do not tell you WHAT the right level is. Determining the right level requires understanding: (1) stockout cost severity for this business, and (2) the structural demand and supply factors specific to the industry segment. A company below the benchmark is not automatically underperforming — it may be holding more inventory for valid operational reasons.
