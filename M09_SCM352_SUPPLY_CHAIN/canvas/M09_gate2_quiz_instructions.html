Answering Each Question Type

  • Fill-in-Blank (FIMB) questions (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6): Type the answer directly into the blank field. Use the exact Chapter 11 term where prompted. Numbers: match the rounding shown in your data (e.g., "14.6" not "14.60").

  • Q3 note: "Upstream" means closer to raw materials, farther from the customer. A decoupling point that shifts upstream is moving away from the customer. Type "above", "at", or "below" for the benchmark comparison blank in Q1.
  • This quiz is auto-graded. Your score is available immediately after submission. Blanks are case-insensitive but must match accepted spellings — do not use abbreviations not shown in the textbook.
