When a Shortage Becomes a Crisis
Supply Chain Management — Structure, Metrics, and Strategy
The Opening Question

During COVID-19, hospitals ran out of PPE within days — not weeks. The products existed. The factories were running. So why did the system fail? The answer is supply chain structure.

This Module Covers
  • How supply chains are structured (tiers, flows)
  • How to measure supply chain performance
  • Why demand signals amplify upstream (bullwhip)
  • How strategy must match demand reality
Case Context

Riverside Health System — a regional hospital network redesigning its supply chain after a shortage crisis exposed structural vulnerabilities in PPE and surgical supply sourcing.
