Supply Chain Tiers: Upstream, Focal Firm, Downstream
Who is in the chain and how they relate to the focal firm
Position Who they are Focal firm's visibility Riverside example
Tier 2 Supplier Supplier's supplier — raw materials and basic inputs Low — often unknown Nitrile rubber grower; resin manufacturer
Tier 1 Supplier Direct supplier — delivers components or finished goods to focal firm High — direct contract Medical glove manufacturer; sterile drape supplier
Focal Firm The organization in focus — manages upstream suppliers and downstream customers Full — internal visibility Riverside Health System
Distributor Intermediate downstream — aggregates and redistributes Medium — contract terms Regional medical supply distributor
End Customer Final recipient of value — generates the demand signal Indirect — demand data only Patients; surgical teams
Why Tier Visibility Matters

A shortage at Tier 2 (raw material) propagates forward through Tier 1 before reaching the focal firm — often with no warning. During COVID-19, nitrile rubber shortages at Tier 2 became glove shortages at the hospital 8–12 weeks later. Focal firms that mapped their Tier 2 suppliers before the crisis had earlier warning and better alternatives.
