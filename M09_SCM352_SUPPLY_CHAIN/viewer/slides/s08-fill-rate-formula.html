Fill Rate: Formula and Interpretation
What fraction of demand is satisfied from stock — not from backorders
Fill Rate Formula
Fill Rate = Orders Filled from Stock ÷ Total Orders Received

Expressed as a percentage. Both terms count order lines, not units, unless otherwise specified.

Worked Example

Riverside Hospital placed 4,200 orders for surgical gloves in Q4. 4,074 were filled from available inventory. 126 required emergency sourcing (not from stock).

Fill Rate = 4,074 ÷ 4,200 = 97.0%

Interpretation: 3% of orders required a stockout response — backorder, emergency purchase, or substitution.

Benchmark Context

Hospital supply fill rates: critical care items >99%; surgical consumables >97%; administrative supplies >95%. Items below category benchmark warrant safety stock review.

Fill Rate vs. Service Level

Fill rate = % of demand units/orders satisfied from stock (continuous measure).

Cycle service level = % of order cycles with no stockout (binary: either the cycle had a stockout or it didn't).

They are not numerically equal. A 97% fill rate does not mean 97% of cycles are stockout-free — a single cycle may have partial fills on multiple orders. This module uses fill rate throughout.
