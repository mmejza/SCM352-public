Cat-A: High Turns ≠ Good Performance

14.6 turns exceeds the 12-turn benchmark, but surgical supplies are high-criticality items. Running lean on critical surgical supplies amplifies stockout risk. High turns here may reflect under-procurement, not operational efficiency.

Cat-B: Low Turns Signals Overcapitalization

120 days of PPE means 4+ months of cash tied up in storage. PPE is bulky and has shelf-life constraints. 3.04 turns vs. a 4-turn benchmark suggests a purchasing pattern that orders too infrequently in large batches — possibly a bullwhip response.
