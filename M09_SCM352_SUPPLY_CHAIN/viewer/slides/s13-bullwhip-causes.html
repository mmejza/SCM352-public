What Causes the Bullwhip?
Four structural causes — each requires a different intervention
Cause Mechanism GloveCo Evidence
Demand Signal Processing Each tier uses past orders (not true demand) to forecast and adds safety stock — errors compound upstream DC forecasted ongoing surge; ordered for 6 weeks at once
Order Batching Firms aggregate demand and place periodic large orders rather than ordering in real-time — creates artificial demand spikes Weeks 5–6 spike followed by zero orders in weeks 8–9
Price Fluctuation Promotions and volume discounts incentivize forward buying — purchases cluster at low-price periods and drop afterward Not visible in this data; check for discount period overlap
Shortage Gaming When supply is tight, buyers inflate orders to receive a larger allocation — leads to phantom demand that evaporates when supply improves DC inflated orders weeks 4–6; cancelled weeks 8–9 when inventory recovered
