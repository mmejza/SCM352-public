Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System operates a network of 18 clinical facilities across the Mountain West. Its central pharmacy fills prescriptions and fulfills supply requisitions for all facilities, including a critical injectable medication used across Riverside's oncology, transplant, and intensive care units. The pharmacy director refers to this medication as Medication R throughout all internal analyses — do the same throughout this quiz.

Riverside's pharmacy orders Medication R from a contracted pharmaceutical distributor. Each time an order is placed, the pharmacy incurs a fixed ordering cost that covers purchasing department labor, distributor order-processing fees, and receiving dock time. Because Medication R requires refrigerated storage, the holding cost includes capital cost tied up in inventory, refrigeration energy, and the risk of expiration before use. The pharmacy tracks annual demand in vials.

You are an operations analyst assisting the pharmacy director. Your version's specific parameters are shown in the version tab tables in Parts 1 and 2 below. Use only your assigned version's numbers throughout — all five parts of the case.
