Formula Quick Reference — Economic Order Quantity

EOQ Formula (Q1)
Q* = √(2KR / h)
K = ordering cost per order · R = annual demand · h = holding cost per unit per year
Total Annual Cost (Q2)
TC = K(R/Q) + h(Q/2) + cR
Three terms: ordering cost + holding cost + purchasing cost · cR does not affect Q* but does affect discount decisions
Orders per Year (Q1b)
N = R / Q*
Time between orders (weeks) = 52 / N
EOQ Cost (Ordering + Holding only)
TC* = √(2KRh)
Useful for Q4 economies of scale — does not include cR
