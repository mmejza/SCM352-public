Step 4 — During-Module Monitoring

Weekly monitoring actions — approx. 30 min total

Day 3
Pull Gate 1 completion report. Students with 0 attempts by Day 3 should receive a targeted nudge (not a class-wide email). Gate 1 is the only entry point — students who miss it cannot access any subsequent content. Typical completion: 85–90% by Day 3.
Day 4
Scan discussion for naming violations. Open a random sample of 10–15 initial posts. Watch specifically for: (1) student naming their employer in the opening line ("I work at Target…"); (2) use of textbook examples — Beer Distributor or Walmart DC are prohibited; (3) posts applying all three failure mode tests identically with generic language (potential AI signal). Flag any prohibited-business posts for resubmission before Day 6.
Day 6
After Gate 2 closes: Export quiz statistics. Check item analysis for Q1 and Q2 — the two most common errors are (a) students omitting the factor of 2 in the EOQ formula (Q* comes out ÷√2 too small) and (b) computing purchasing cost as c × Q instead of c × R. If either error rate exceeds 25%, add a debrief announcement before returning scores. Verify that no student submitted Gate 2 without submitting the Case.
Post-module
Grade Discussion initial posts after the following module closes. Use M10_Discussion_Rubric.html for criterion-by-criterion scoring. Expected grading time: 2–4 min per student. Apply 10-point naming penalty before entering rubric scores. Discussion does not require reply posts — grade initial only.
