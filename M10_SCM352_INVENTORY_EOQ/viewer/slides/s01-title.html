The $800 Million Mistake
Inventory Management and the Economic Order Quantity
What Went Wrong

In 2020–2021, hospitals ordered two to three years of PPE, IV bags, and critical medications. The ordering cost was immediate and visible. The holding cost — capital tied up, storage space, deterioration, expiration write-offs — was invisible and deferred.

When the holding cost arrived, it arrived all at once. Fourteen health systems wrote off an estimated $800 million in expired and obsolete supplies.

This Module

Make the invisible holding cost visible — then optimize the tradeoff between ordering cost and holding cost using the EOQ model.

What You Will Build

Formula intuition · TC decomposition · economies of scale · quantity discount analysis · pallet constraints · model limits
