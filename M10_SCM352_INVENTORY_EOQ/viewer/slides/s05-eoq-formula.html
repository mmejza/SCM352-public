The EOQ Formula
Q* = √(2KR/h) — parameter identification and units
Q* = √( 2KR / h )
Variable Definitions

Q* — optimal order quantity (units per order)

K — fixed ordering cost ($/order); includes processing, shipping, setup

R — annual demand (units/year); must be in consistent time units

h — annual holding cost per unit ($/unit/year); includes capital cost, storage, insurance, obsolescence

Derived Quantities

Orders per year = R / Q*

Time between orders = 52 weeks / (R/Q*) = 52Q*/R

Average inventory = Q*/2 units

Common Errors

Forgetting the factor of 2 in the numerator

Using weekly demand when h is annual — keep units consistent

Reporting Q* without rounding to practical integer units
