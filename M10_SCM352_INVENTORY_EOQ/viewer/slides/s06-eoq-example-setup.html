Scenario

A medical supply distributor sells surgical gloves at a rate of R = 4,800 units/year. Each order incurs a fixed ordering cost of K = $75. The annual holding cost is h = $6.00 per unit per year.

Part (a)

Write the EOQ formula, identify each variable, and compute Q*. Show all steps.

Part (b)

How many orders will the firm place per year at Q*? What is the time between orders in weeks?

Part (c)

The firm currently orders in batches of 200 units. Is it ordering too frequently, not frequently enough, or near the optimal level? Explain using your Q* result.
