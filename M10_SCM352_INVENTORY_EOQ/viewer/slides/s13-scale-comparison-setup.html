Work through all three parts. The economies of scale pattern from the previous slide should predict the key result before you compute — verify that prediction numerically.

Scenario

Two regional grocery chains both sell the same bottled water. Unit purchase cost c = $0.50/bottle. Fixed ordering cost K = $30/order. Annual holding cost h = $0.10/bottle/year.

NorthMart

R = 90,000 bottles/year

SouthMart

R = 360,000 bottles/year

Parts (a) and (b)

(a) Compute Q* and total annual EOQ cost (ordering + holding only, excluding cR) for both chains.

(b) SouthMart's demand is 4× NorthMart's. By what factor does EOQ cost increase? Does this confirm the √2 rule?

Part (c)

Compute EOQ cost per unit (total EOQ cost ÷ R) for each chain. Which has lower per-unit cost? What is the managerial implication?
