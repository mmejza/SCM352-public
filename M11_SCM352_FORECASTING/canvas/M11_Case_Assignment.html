Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System operates a network of 18 clinical facilities across the Mountain West. Its central pharmacy fills prescriptions and fulfills supply requisitions for all facilities across several high-volume supply categories. The pharmacy director has asked you — as the operations analyst — to evaluate and implement a formal forecasting system for one of these supply categories, assigned by your version.

Riverside currently tracks weekly demand but has been forecasting informally. The director wants to compare two statistical methods — moving average and exponential smoothing — and evaluate how they perform alongside expert-based judgment for a new product introduction. Your analysis will directly inform inventory ordering decisions in the modules ahead.

Continuity note: This case extends the Riverside Health System scenario from Modules 2–11. The MAD and MAPE you compute are the statistical inputs to safety stock sizing covered in Module 12. Record your computed values before closing the quiz.
