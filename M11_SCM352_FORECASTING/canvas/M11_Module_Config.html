Step 4 — During-Module Monitoring

Weekly monitoring actions — 30 min total

Day 3
Pull Gate 1 completion report. The MAE=MAD equivalence (G1-Q5) and "Level" as a fourth demand component (G1-Q2) are chapter-exclusive and not in the slides. Students with 0 Gate 1 attempts by Day 3 need a prompt — they cannot access any graded content until they pass Gate 1.
Day 4
Scan discussion initial posts. Watch for: (a) posts that use the Cisco 2000 case — this is the prohibited example from the module overview; send resubmission request. (b) posts that describe the failure in general terms without identifying a specific demand component — look for vocabulary from Chapter 15 (level, trend, seasonality, randomness). (c) posts describing "high bias" but computing signed vs. unsigned errors incorrectly in their diagnosis section.
Day 6
After Gate 2 closes: Export quiz statistics. Common error patterns to watch: Q1 window bound errors (students include the current period's forecast in the MA calculation); Q2 rounding errors in the ES chain; Q3 bias sign confusion (students compute |errors| for bias instead of signed errors); Q6 denominator errors in MAPE (students divide by forecast instead of actual demand); Q7 confusion of anchoring vs. overconfidence — anchoring requires naming the structural trigger (preliminary estimate presented first).
Post-module
Grade Discussion initial posts after following-module close. See M11_Discussion_Rubric.html. Grading note: posts that correctly identify forecast gaming as the bias in the Part 4 scenario (budget controller under-forecasting) earn full credit for Part 3 (Error Type) even if the rest of Part 4 terminology is imprecise — the gaming identification is the primary diagnostic signal.
