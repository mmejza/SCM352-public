Every Forecast Is Wrong
The question is whether errors are random — or systematic
The Fundamental Problem

No forecasting model produces the correct number. Actual demand always differs from the forecast. The operational question is not whether your forecast will be wrong — it will be. The question is whether errors are random and unpredictable or systematic and correctable.

Random Error (MAD)

Errors scatter around zero — some high, some low. Buffer stock absorbs this. The model is centered correctly; the world is just noisy.

Systematic Error (Bias)

Errors consistently run in one direction. Buffer stock does not fix this — the model is miscalibrated. This requires diagnosis and recalibration.
