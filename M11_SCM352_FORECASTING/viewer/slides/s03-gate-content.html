Read Chapter 15 Before Gate 1

Gate 1 draws exclusively on content in Chapter 15 that is not covered in the slide deck or lecture videos. Slides and videos alone are not sufficient to pass Gate 1.

Time-Series vs. Causal Methods

Chapter 15 defines the fundamental distinction between methods that use only historical demand data (time-series) and those that incorporate external drivers (causal). This framing is chapter-level analysis.

MAE = MAD (Explicit Equivalence)

Chapter 15, Section 15.2 explicitly states that Mean Absolute Error (MAE) "is also referred to as the mean absolute deviation (MAD)." The two terms are mathematically identical. This equivalence is stated in the chapter text only.

Forecast Biases — Structural Analysis

Chapter 15.6 analyzes overconfidence, anchoring, and forecast gaming. The chapter explains the structural incentive condition that makes gaming predictable — analysis absent from the slide deck.

Moving Average Lag — The Mechanism

Chapter 15 explains mechanistically why MA lags a true trend: equal weighting of all n observations pulls the forecast toward the midpoint of the window, not the current level.
