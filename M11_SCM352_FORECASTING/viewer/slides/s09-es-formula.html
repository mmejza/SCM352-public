Exponential Smoothing Formula
Ft+1 = α · Dt + (1−α) · Ft — weighted blend of observation and history
Ft+1 = α · Dt + (1−α) · Ft
Variable Definitions

Ft+1 — forecast for the next period

α — smoothing parameter; 0 < α < 1

Dt — actual demand observed in period t

Ft — forecast that was made for period t (previous forecast)

F1 — initial forecast; set by the analyst as a starting value

The α Trade-Off

High α (→1.0): new forecast ≈ most recent demand. Very responsive — tracks trend fast but amplifies noise.

Low α (→0.0): new forecast ≈ previous forecast. Very smooth — resists noise but lags behind true changes.

Recursive Structure

Each Ft depends on the Ft−1 before it. The chain runs forward from F1. Never replace Ft with Dt−1.
