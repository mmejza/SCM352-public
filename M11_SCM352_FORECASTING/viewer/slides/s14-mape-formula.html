The MAPE Formula
Mean Absolute Percentage Error — scales accuracy relative to demand volume
MAPE = (1/n) × Σ ( |Dt − Ft| / Dt ) × 100%
Why MAPE — Not Just MAD

MAD is in the same units as demand. A MAD of 15 units means something different for a product selling 50 units/week vs. 5,000 units/week. MAPE normalizes error as a percentage of actual demand, making accuracy comparable across products at different volume levels.

MAD = 15 on demand of 50 → 30% error. MAD = 15 on demand of 5,000 → 0.3% error. Only MAPE reveals which model is actually more accurate.

Per-Period Calculation

For each period t:

APEt = |Dt − Ft| / Dt × 100%

Then MAPE = average of all APEt values.

Critical: Denominator = Actual

Divide by Dt (actual demand), not Ft (forecast). Use absolute values in the numerator — MAPE is always positive.
