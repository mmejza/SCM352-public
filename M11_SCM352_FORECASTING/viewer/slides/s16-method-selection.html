Method Selection — MA vs. ES
No method dominates all situations — demand pattern drives the choice
Demand PatternRecommended Method / ParameterWhy
Stable, noisy
Low variance around a flat mean; random spikes		 Long MA (n = 8–12) or low α (0.1–0.2) Smoothing is the priority — averaging many periods (or giving low weight to any single observation) dampens random noise
Consistently trending
Systematic upward or downward movement		 Short MA (n = 2–3) or high α (0.5–0.7) Responsiveness is the priority — the forecast must track the current level rather than lag behind older values
Step change
Sudden permanent shift to a new level		 High α ES strongly preferred ES with high α converges to the new level in 3–4 periods; a long MA window drags along the old level for n periods
Seasonal
Repeating annual or cycle pattern		 Neither MA nor basic ES — see note Both methods fail to detect repeating seasonal structure; see Slide 17
