When Neither Method Works
Recognizing the structural limits of MA and exponential smoothing
Chapter 15 — Explicit Limitation

The chapter states that naive, moving average, and simple exponential smoothing methods all "fail to detect systemic variation in the data." Two specific patterns expose this failure.

Failure Mode 1: Consistent Trend

When demand rises 50 units per period, every MA and basic ES forecast lags behind. Shortening n or raising α reduces lag but never eliminates it — the method has no mechanism to project a trend forward.

Diagnostic signal: consistent positive bias in the error analysis, combined with a visibly rising demand plot.

Correct response: recognize the trend — parameter tuning will not solve a structural mismatch.

Failure Mode 2: Repeating Seasonality

When demand spikes every Q4 and drops every Q1, neither method can anticipate the seasonal swing. They will perpetually under-forecast in peaks and over-forecast in troughs.

Diagnostic signal: error analysis alternates between large positive errors (peak seasons) and large negative errors (off-seasons) — never settling into a low-MAD range.

Correct response: the demand series has seasonal structure — a different method family is required.
