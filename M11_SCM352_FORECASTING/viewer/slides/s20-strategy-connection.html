Module Synthesis — The Complete Toolkit
Statistical methods + judgmental methods + accuracy metrics
Statistical Methods (Historical Data)

Moving Average: stable demand; select n based on responsiveness vs. smoothing trade-off

Exponential Smoothing: trending demand; select α based on how quickly the true level changes

Accuracy Metrics: MAD (magnitude), Bias (direction), MAPE (scale-normalized) — use all three together to diagnose model quality

Judgmental Methods (No/Limited Data)

Forecast Combination: independent expert aggregation for new products

Delphi: structured iterative expert consensus without anchoring risk

Analogical: comparable historical analog when internal history is unavailable

Watch for: overconfidence, anchoring, and forecast gaming in any judgmental process
