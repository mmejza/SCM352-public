Background — All Versions

Riverside Health System operates a single general surgery operating room at a community hospital. The OR runs Monday–Friday, 7 AM–3 PM (8 hours = 480 minutes per day). The OR scheduling coordinator currently assigns procedures to the next available OR slot in the order they are booked — a first-come, first-served (FCFS) approach.

The OR team is evaluating its scheduling approach. The scheduling coordinator believes that switching to a priority-based sequencing system would reduce average case turnaround time and better protect urgent cases from experiencing delays. You have been hired as an operations analyst. Your task is to evaluate three alternative sequencing rules — SPT, WSPT, and EDD — and recommend a sequencing approach for the Riverside OR.

Setup for all versions:

You are given a set of five procedures scheduled for a particular Monday OR block. All procedures are available to be scheduled at 7 AM. The OR handles one procedure at a time; there are no setup times between procedures.

  • Processing time: estimated surgical duration in minutes
  • Urgency weight: clinical priority score (1 = routine; 2 = elevated; 3 = urgent; 4 = emergent)
  • Deadline: hours from 7 AM by which the procedure must be completed (e.g., 3.0 hrs = by 10:00 AM = 180 min)
