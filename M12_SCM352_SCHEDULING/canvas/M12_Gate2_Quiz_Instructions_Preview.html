Answering Each Item Type

Fill-in-the-Blank items (Q1–Q4 calculation parts)
Type your answer directly into each blank field. Canvas accepts common formatting variants for sequences (e.g., B-D-A-E-C or B,D,A,E,C). Work out your complete table on scratch paper first — enter values only when you have the full table in front of you. Do not leave a blank empty and plan to return; partial saves are not reliable.
Tardiness trap — applies to all sequencing items
Tardiness = max(0, Lateness). If lateness is negative (the job finishes early), tardiness is zero — not the lateness value. There is no negative tardiness. Entering a negative number in a tardiness blank will score as wrong.
