What Is Scheduling?
Assigning work to resources at specific points in time — and why order matters
Scheduling vs. Capacity Planning

Capacity planning asks: How much resource should we have? Scheduling asks: In what order do we use the resource we have? Scheduling assumes capacity is fixed and manages how it is deployed.

Flow Time

Total elapsed time from when a job enters the system to when it is completed. Flow time = wait time + processing time. A job's flow time is determined by every job that runs before it.

Why Sequence Changes Performance

Two jobs: X = 3 days, Y = 7 days. Same total work either way.

Key Insight

Every job that follows a job inherits that job's processing time as part of its wait. Put short jobs early — distribute the wait burden efficiently.
