SPT — Shortest Processing Time
Sequence ascending by processing time · Minimizes average flow time
The Rule

Sequence jobs from shortest to longest processing time. Do the quickest job first.

What It Minimizes

Average flow time across all jobs. Also minimizes average work-in-process inventory. Provably optimal — not just a heuristic.

What It Ignores

Due dates. Job importance. Revenue. Urgency. All jobs are treated as equally valuable.

Why It Works — The Intuition

Every job that follows another job inherits its processing time as wait. A long job first forces every subsequent job to absorb that duration into its own flow time. A short job first minimizes the inherited wait for all jobs that come after it. SPT distributes the waiting burden most efficiently across the whole queue.

Documented Limitation — Job Starvation

Long jobs can be repeatedly bumped behind shorter jobs indefinitely. Managers often use SPT as a default and manually override it for jobs that have waited too long or have tight due dates.
