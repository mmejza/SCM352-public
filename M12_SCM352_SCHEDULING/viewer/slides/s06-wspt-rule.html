WSPT — Weighted Shortest Processing Time
Sequence descending by weight/processing-time ratio · Minimizes total weighted flow time
WSPT Ratio  =  Weight  ÷  Processing Time   |   Sequence: DESCENDING (highest ratio first)
Why Weights?

SPT treats all jobs equally. WSPT assigns each job a weight — revenue, urgency, strategic priority — and ensures high-weight jobs finish sooner regardless of processing time alone.

Why Descending?

High ratio = high weight AND/OR short processing time. Both properties make a job desirable to complete early. Descending ratio = highest combined desirability first.

What It Minimizes

Total weighted flow time = Σ (weight × flow time). This ensures that the jobs you care most about — high weight — complete with the smallest total flow time cost.

Real-World Application — Emergency Severity Index

Hospital ERs assign ESI scores 1–5 (1 = resuscitation, 5 = non-urgent). ESI is effectively a WSPT weight. A chest-pain patient (ESI 1) is treated before a minor laceration (ESI 4) not because the chest-pain visit is shorter, but because its weight is high enough that the ratio dominates.
