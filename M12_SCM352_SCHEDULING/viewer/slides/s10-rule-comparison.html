Three Rules, Three Objectives
No rule dominates on all metrics — choose based on what your organization actually measures
Rule Sequence By Provably Minimizes Does NOT Control Best When
SPT Proc. time ↑ Average flow time · Avg WIP Due dates · Job priority Throughput speed is the primary metric; all jobs roughly equal value
WSPT W÷P ratio ↓ Total weighted flow time Maximum tardiness Jobs have meaningfully different priorities (revenue, urgency, strategic weight)
EDD Due date ↑ Maximum tardiness · Max lateness Average flow time One late delivery is catastrophic; deadline adherence dominates all other concerns
Common Hybrid Practice

Apply EDD to identify at-risk jobs. Within the safe subset, use SPT to minimize average flow time. This captures EDD's deadline protection without sacrificing throughput on non-urgent work.

What Rules Cannot Do

No rule eliminates variability in processing times or demand arrivals. Rules choose the best possible sequence given fixed inputs — they do not change the inputs themselves.
