Choosing a Sequencing Rule
Three diagnostic questions · Fairness considerations
Q1 — What are you minimizing?

Average throughput time? → SPT
Weighted throughput (important customers)? → WSPT
Worst-case delay? → EDD

Q2 — How reliable are due dates?

EDD requires trustworthy due dates. If salespeople mark every job "urgent" to win priority, EDD is gamed and loses its value. SPT is more robust — only processing time estimates needed.

Q3 — Do jobs differ in importance?

If all jobs are roughly equal in value: SPT — simpler, more defensible. If jobs have clear priority differences: WSPT — captures the structure that matters.

Fairness — An Organizational Question, Not Just a Technical One

SPT: documented risk of starvation — long jobs wait indefinitely unless manually overridden.  |  EDD: natural fairness — a job gains priority as its deadline approaches.  |  WSPT: explicitly favors high-weight customers — which may or may not be acceptable to all stakeholders. The choice of rule encodes organizational values, not just optimization preferences.
