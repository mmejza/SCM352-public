Theory of Constraints
Identifying the bottleneck · Implied utilization · Why only the bottleneck limits throughput
Implied Utilization  =  Demand Rate  ÷  Capacity   |   Bottleneck = Resource with Highest Implied Utilization

System demand: 200 units/week. System ceiling: 210 units/week — set by Step 2.

The Core Claim

Investing in Step 1 or Step 3 — doubling their capacity — does not change the system output ceiling. Only improving the bottleneck (Step 2) increases throughput. Non-bottleneck investments produce only WIP inventory.

Two Corollary Prescriptions

1. Protect the bottleneck — idle time at the bottleneck = permanently lost output. Idle time at a non-bottleneck = buffer only.

2. Let the bottleneck set the pace — running non-bottlenecks faster than the bottleneck can absorb only creates excess WIP inventory.
