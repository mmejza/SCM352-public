Appointment Systems
Managing the timing of arrivals — not capacity itself
What Appointment Systems Do — and Do Not Do

An appointment system does not add capacity. It spreads arrivals across time to reduce the mismatch between supply and demand at any given moment. Without appointments: random arrivals cluster — overwhelmed one hour, idle the next. With appointments: arrivals are smoothed, matching the pace of available service capacity.

Shorter Intervals (less than avg service time)

+ Higher provider utilization
− Backlog builds through the day
− Later patients wait longer
Trade-off: efficiency over responsiveness

Longer Intervals (more than avg service time)

+ Very short wait times for all patients
− Provider has idle time between patients
− Lower utilization
Trade-off: responsiveness over efficiency

Service Variability Complication

If service times vary significantly (routine visit = 10 min; complex consultation = 45 min), slots sized to the average leave the provider idle on short visits and create delays on long visits. Solution: triage appointments by type and assign slot lengths accordingly.
