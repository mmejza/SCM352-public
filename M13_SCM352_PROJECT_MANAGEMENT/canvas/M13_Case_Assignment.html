Project Background — 10 Activities

Riverside Health System is replacing its legacy patient records system with a new Electronic Health Records (EHR) platform across all three campuses. The project involves 10 activities: A (project planning and vendor finalization), B (infrastructure build-out and server installation), C (vendor-led EHR software configuration), D (data migration and historical record conversion), E (integration testing — requires C and D complete), F (staff training — requires B and E complete), G (go-live readiness audit — requires D complete), H (pilot campus rollout — requires E, F, and G complete), I (issue remediation post-pilot — requires H complete), and J (full system cutover and sign-off — requires I complete).

The dependency structure above is the same for all four versions. Your version's duration values are shown in the activity table at the top of the Canvas quiz. Use those durations throughout all four parts.

Recommended scratch-paper sequence:
  1. List activities A–J, record your version's durations from the Canvas table.
  2. Draw the AON network; confirm merge points.
  3. Forward pass: compute EST and ECT for every activity .
  4. Record project duration.
  5. Backward pass: compute LCT and LST.
  6. Compute ST for every activity.
  7. List all 6 paths A→J and verify the critical path.
