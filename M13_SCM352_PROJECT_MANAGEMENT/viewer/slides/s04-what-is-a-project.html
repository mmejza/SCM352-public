What Is a Project?
A temporary collection of interdependent activities with a defined end goal
The Fundamental Problem

Projects fail not because activities are impossible — but because their dependencies are misunderstood. Activities started before their predecessors are complete create rework. Delays cascade through the network. The minimum project duration is determined by the longest chain of dependent activities.

The Three Questions

1. What activities are required, and what order are they in?

2. What is the minimum possible project duration?

3. Which activities have zero flexibility — where any delay extends the deadline?

The Three-Tool CPM Framework

Dependency MatrixComplete list of activities and their predecessor relationships
