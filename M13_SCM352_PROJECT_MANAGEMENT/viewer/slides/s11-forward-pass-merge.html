Forward Pass — The Merge Calculation
At every merge point, EST = max of all predecessor ECTs
Why MAX — The Intuition

An activity with two predecessors is like a meeting that requires two people to arrive. The meeting cannot start until both have arrived. If one arrives at minute 9 and the other at minute 14, the meeting starts at minute 14 — not the average, not the minimum.

The successor activity is waiting in the lobby. The last predecessor to finish is the one that lets it in.

The Most Common Error

Using the AVERAGE or MINIMUM instead of MAX. Students see ECT(C)=7 and ECT(D)=11 and compute EST(E)=9 (average) or EST(E)=7 (minimum). Both are wrong.

Correct: EST(E) = max(7, 11) = 11

E cannot start until both C and D are done. D is the binding constraint.

Restaurant Expansion — Activity E

E (Grand opening) requires both C and D to be complete.

EST(E) = max(ECT(C), ECT(D)) = max(7, 11) = 11

ECT(E) = EST(E) + Duration(E) = 11 + 1 = 12

Even though staff training (C) finishes at week 7, the grand opening cannot happen until the POS system is installed at week 11. C's completion alone is not enough.
