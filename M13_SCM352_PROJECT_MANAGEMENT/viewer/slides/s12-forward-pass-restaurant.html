Key Calculations

A is the first activity — EST(A) = 0.

B and C both follow A — they can both start at week 3. EST(B) = EST(C) = ECT(A) = 3.

D follows B — EST(D) = ECT(B) = 9. ECT(D) = 9 + 2 = 11.

E follows both C and D — this is the merge point:

EST(E) = max(ECT(C), ECT(D)) = max(7, 11) = 11

C finishes at week 7 — 4 weeks before E can start. C "waits" for D to finish. This waiting period becomes C's slack, computed in the backward pass.
