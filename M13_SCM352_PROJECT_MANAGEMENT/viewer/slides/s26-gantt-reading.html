Gantt Chart — Reading the Chart
Solid bar = activity duration (EST to ECT) · Dashed bar = slack window (ECT to LCT)
Two Types of Bars

Solid bar: Spans from EST to ECT. Represents the actual duration of the activity. All activities have one.

Dashed bar (slack window): Spans from ECT to LCT. Represents how far right the activity can shift without extending the project. Only non-critical activities (ST > 0) have one.

Critical activities: Solid bar only — ECT = LCT, so there is no slack window to show.
